# The Orrery

The Orrery is the instrument that makes a system legible without pretending
to reproduce it. This campaign deepened Hornvale's astronomy from a handful
of circular placeholders into a deterministic historical sky: a hierarchical
Keplerian substrate, persistent comets, observer-specific meteor showers, a
modeled neighborhood of stars, and a lazy naked-eye background.

The sky is not one universal picture. A species' activity cycle, night vision,
and sky attention shape the candidates available to an observer, while local
rotation, twilight, atmosphere, moonlight, and horizon geometry shape the
actual view. The physical candidates remain stable identities, so cultures
can group and name the same visible stars differently without astronomy
owning their meanings.

The campaign deliberately stops short of N-body integration, tidal evolution,
stellar luminosity drift, guaranteed stellar catastrophes, dense meteor clumps,
eclipsing binaries, and terminal stellar evolution. Those are future physical
questions, not hidden promises in this slice.
