# The Generalist

The roster held five peoples, and every one of them had a refuge. The
highlander held the mountain, the wet lowlander held the swamp forest, the
open-ground dweller held the plains, the arid-margin dweller held the desert —
and the coexistence packer that divides a cell's carrying capacity among
whoever competes for it had, in five peoples' worth of testing, never once had
to arbitrate against a competitor with nowhere it alone could stand. This
campaign built one: a sixth people, authored wide and unexcluded on every
condition axis and least devoted to any of them, and then asked whether the
packer's tuning survives meeting it. Humans are not a setting detail here.
They are the experiment's instrument.

## Wide is not what it sounds like

The honest reading of "no refuge" turned out to have a trap folded into it.
The model that scores how well a creature fits a place multiplies a shape term
by a strength term — how far a curve reaches, and how hard it insists on its
centre — and the strength term is not an independent knob. It scales the
curve's whole output, so a wide-but-weak niche and a narrow-but-weak niche can
put a creature in the same place on the same measurement for two entirely
different reasons. The niche this campaign first shipped was authored to the
sound of "widest, least devoted" but had, on two of four axes, actually
shipped *narrower* than the roster's other generalist — an argued half
(devotion, deliberately low) sitting next to an unargued half (width, never
checked against anything). The two pulled in opposite directions, and the
distinctness measurement that was supposed to prove the human niche a genuine
break from the existing generalist's came back carried mostly by that
unargued width, not by the devotion contrast the design was actually about.

The repair measured the land a body would actually stand on — the
distribution of temperature, moisture, light and elevation across thirty
worlds' worth of settleable ground — and set each axis's width to the
smallest value that still let the response vary by no more than a fifth of
its peak across the middle ninety percent of that distribution, raised further
where needed to clear every other people's own width on that axis. Elevation
already cleared its floor by a wide margin and was left alone at its authored
optimum. Once every axis passed both tests, width and devotion stopped
disagreeing about which way the contrast pointed, and the measurement that
follows in this chronicle is the one taken after that repair, not before it.

## The reference vector, met on purpose

A separate design decision this campaign inherited rather than invented: an
earlier campaign had removed the project's one hidden convention, that a
species compared against a neutral reference on every psychological,
perceptual and vocal dimension was in fact being compared against one
particular people's authored values. That repair made it *legal* for a new
people to land exactly on the reference rather than merely near it, and
humans do, on the one axis that measures the impulse to stand and fight rather
than flee. That is not a coincidence dodged; it is the argued position — the
reference vector already sits at what a genuinely unremarkable temperament
looks like, and a species built to be the roster's least-committed competitor
belongs there.

It has a consequence worth stating rather than discovering later: a raiding
rule elsewhere in the world gates initiative on that same dimension, and a
value at the reference sits below its threshold. One other people already
declined to raid for the same reason; humans make two. Nothing about either
number moved to cause or avoid this — it is what authoring a temperament at
its argued value produces, disclosed rather than patched, and it surfaced a
question about what that gate is actually measuring that this campaign
correctly left for a different piece of work.

Night vision was the one dimension this campaign chose rather than merely
computed. Humans see poorly in the dark by any reasonable reading — worse
than everything else in the world that speaks — and the model's colour
vocabulary is a step function of exactly that scalar, so the choice was never
really about the number; it was about which of two adjacent steps a reader
would find defensible. The wider step ties humans with the world's other
warm-marginal generalist. The narrower one gives humans the shallowest colour
vocabulary anyone speaks, alone. This campaign took the narrower step,
on the argument that a colour ladder built from human languages should not
then deny humans its own deepest rung — and humans become the first witness
to a rung nothing had reached before.

## The vacuity check, and what it is not allowed to prove

Before any of the three predictions below could mean anything, this campaign
owed a narrower one: that a human is not merely the existing generalist
wearing a new name. The first version of that check turned out to be
unbuildable as originally worded — the two generalists share two axis optima
by deliberate design, and a fit measurement is mathematically incapable of
distinguishing two curves that agree on where their peaks sit, no matter what
either curve's width or strength is authored to. The check that replaced it
compares the two species' fit values by their spread rather than their rank,
and clears a floor once the width repair above made the two component
readings agree in direction rather than fight. A companion test proves the
check is not vacuous the ordinary way a green suite can be: swapping the human
niche for the other generalist's, unmodified, turns the check red. A passing
suite proves the code ran. Only that swap proves the axis is visible to it at
all.

## The reading, once the swap and the repair both held

Three predictions were frozen before any of this was measured, against the
five peoples already in the world.

**The first — that humans would compete hardest with the roster's existing
generalist, more than with any other pair in the world — held decisively.**
Of fifteen possible pairings among the six peoples, humans and the other
generalist ranked first on both of two independent measurements: how much
their fitted suitability rises and falls together across settleable land, and
how much their share of any given cell's carrying capacity tracks together.
Both margins over the second-place pair were wide, not marginal.

**The second — that humans would not take a specialist's own stronghold away
from it — held on every band this campaign could actually test.** Humans were
never the best-fit people, and never held a cell majority, on the highland
ground, the wet lowland, or the open plains each specialist's own authored
niche claims as home. One stronghold could not be tested at all: the
literal predicate for the desert-dwelling specialist's ground, read directly
off its own documentation, matched not one settleable cell across thirty
worlds. That is a finding about an existing people's authored ground, not
about humans, and it is recorded rather than quietly worked around.

**The third — whether humans would take over most of the land, including the
strongholds, and expose the coexistence tuning as untested against a genuine
generalist — did not fire, but not for the reason a first reading suggests.**
Humans held a majority share on not one of a hundred forty-two thousand
settleable cells anywhere in the sample. That would read, on its own, as the
tuning surviving contact with exactly the competitor built to stress it. It
does not survive contact with the control this campaign added alongside it:
the same test, applied to the roster's strongest, most locally dominant
specialist, in that specialist's own best cells, also never crosses the
threshold. Its own maximum share anywhere in the sample sits at roughly a
third of the way there — barely above what six equally-matched
competitors splitting a cell evenly would each get. The threshold this
campaign's third prediction was built against turns out to be unreachable by
anything in a six-way contest at this scale, which means a null result against
it is not, by itself, evidence about the generalist specifically. It is
evidence about the instrument.

## The finding underneath the finding

The strength term that scales a species' whole response curve is the same
number, whichever direction a creature has been authored: wide-and-weak and
narrow-and-weak both cap out below wide-and-strong. That fact was already
present in the model before this campaign; what this campaign adds is that it
makes a certain kind of generalist impossible to author, not merely difficult.
"No refuge" was operationalised here as the widest curves in the roster
*and* the least committed to any of them — and both halves of that choice
point the same way. Width removes any axis a competitor could be excluded
from. Low commitment caps that competitor's own ceiling on every axis at
the same moment. A creature that is simultaneously everywhere and strong
cannot be built this way, and so was not, and could not have been, tested
here.

What was tested, honestly stated, is narrower than the question this campaign
set out to answer: the coexistence tuning survived a competitor that is broad
and weak. Whether it survives a competitor that is broad and strong — a
generalist authored nearer one specialist's own high level of commitment to
its ground, but spread across every axis rather than one — remains open, and
building one is not free: nothing in the roster today instantiates a wide,
strongly-committed niche, and authoring one deliberately, to close this gap,
is future work rather than a footnote.

A second, smaller finding sits beside the headline. The two generalists
occupy almost exactly the same ground — first of fifteen pairs, by a wide
margin, on both measurements of overlap — and across that shared ground the
older generalist still out-competes the purpose-built one on raw fitted
suitability and on competitive share, everywhere the comparison was
aggregated. Humans rank second-lowest of the six peoples on both statistics,
ahead only of the desert-dwelling specialist whose own stronghold could not be
evaluated. Being everywhere is not the same as thriving anywhere, and this
campaign's own generalist demonstrates the difference against the roster's
other one.

## No discontinuity was declared, and here is why

A sixth settling people changes what the settlement packer iterates over,
and regenerating every committed artifact after this campaign moved a very
large amount of text — placement, population, and every generated name across
the reference worlds changed, because the packer resolves each cell's outcome
against the *entire* roster competing for it, and a new competitor changes
that resolution everywhere, not only where it wins. That is real, visible
movement, and it would be easy to mistake for the kind of change that owes a
formal discontinuity in how a saved world is understood.

It is not that kind of change. Every seed-derivation label this project
defines — the named, permanent contracts a saved world's provenance is
checked against — was inspected end to end across this campaign's full
history, and not one changed shape, changed order, or changed what it reads.
The world's own record of which version of which algorithm produced it is
unchanged, verified rather than assumed, both before and after the niche
repair described above. What moved was the population an existing, unchanged
algorithm is asked to arbitrate between — the same shape of change every
earlier addition to the roster has produced, and no addition before this one
has needed a formal discontinuity for that reason either. A discontinuity is
owed when an algorithm starts reading something differently, not when it is
asked to read something new. Nothing here reads differently.

## What the roster now exercises

Six peoples share the landscape where five did, and the world's account of
who competes for a given patch of ground has one more voice in it than it
used to. The colour vocabulary has a witness at its deepest rung for the
first time. And a limitation this project did not know it had — that its
model of ecological breadth cannot separate being everywhere from being
weak — is now written down, measured, and waiting on whoever next wants to
build the competitor that would actually test it.
