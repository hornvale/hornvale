# The Social Household

*A household is not a universal shape. It is a projection of people, relations,
care, resources, authority, and time.*

The Grammar gave Hornvale a reproductive substrate without turning biology into
social destiny. This campaign built the next seam: a small, test-backed social
substrate that can carry sex traits, reproductive roles, identity, association,
kinship, care, descent, inheritance, movement, and lifecycle change without
pretending that any one of those is the definition of a family.

## Cohorts before biographies

The substrate has two levels. Aggregate cohorts carry distributions and
topologies: reproductive roles, dependency and care, descent possibilities,
compatibility, identity and recognition possibilities, relation counts, and
lifecycle rates. Realization is a later projection. It derives person records,
directed relations, lifecycle events, and group projections from an explicit
cohort context and a seed, with provenance on every emitted fact.

That boundary matters. A cohort can say that a population permits several
parentage pathways without saying which person is someone's parent. A person
can have sex traits, social gender, identity, and reproductive role as distinct
facts. A group can be a dwelling, a care group, a work group, a ritual group,
or a legal association without becoming the world's one true household.

## A vocabulary, not a family template

The shipped vocabulary is deliberately small: person, group, relation,
association, care, descent, inheritance, migration, recomposition, and
lifecycle event. Relations are directed and typed; their endpoints and
validity intervals remain visible. Group projections are derived from facts and
may overlap or change over time. Dissolution does not erase history, and death
does not erase a relation; it changes what future events can occur.

Material conditions enter as context rather than household-internal law.
Subsistence, property, mobility, authority, religion, contact, and population
pressure can change which associations are viable, recognized, or costly. The
social layer does not hard-code their consequences, and it does not import
real-world racial, ethnic, homophobic, transphobic, antisemitic, or
white-supremacist structures as setting defaults.

## The Lot can speak, carefully

The Lot now reads realized facts only through explicit identity and life-time
boundaries. It can report sourced sex or reproductive-role facts, gender or
identity facts, association and descent, children and siblings, adoption and
care, migration and recomposition, and parental death or inheritance when the
projection actually supplies them. Missing evidence remains honest silence;
biology is never used as a proxy for identity, partnership, or social meaning.

The implementation is additive and opt-in. Existing canonical species and
world bytes acquire no new social canon. Synthetic probe societies exercise
pair, brood, care, adoption, dissolution, migration, recomposition, and death
cases without naming new Hornvale peoples. Full institutions, economy,
religion, magic, anatomy, species taxonomy, and the broader social graph remain
future consumers of this seam.
