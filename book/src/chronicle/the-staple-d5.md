# The Staple D5

*A city is not a large place. It is a place where many routes meet, and the
question is whether the world already makes one without being told to.*

D5 built the instrument for that question. It reads the flows and relations
already produced by D2 and D4, comparing each living settlement with its peers.
It keeps the evidence typed: source identities, directions, phase identity,
voluntary and coercive provenance, recurrence, and the controls that could
otherwise fool the reading—population, density, throughput, catchment, age,
and relation count.

## A detector, not a city

The new diagnostic can recognize convergent flow profiles, but it does not
make a settlement into a city. It assigns no `Seat`, `Notability`, `Function`,
occupation, specialization, production rule, movement rule, epoch, or saved
world fact. A big isolated settlement and a coercive hub can therefore be
reported as failures of the interpretation rather than mistaken for an apex.

Missing joins, unavailable channels, malformed or duplicate evidence, zero
flows, isolated settlements, and incomplete windows remain explicit branches.
The live reducer joins existing D4 witnesses by stable identity and evaluates
each seed independently, so a pooled total cannot rescue an underpowered
world.

## What shipped

D5 shipped its pure comparative-apex model, deterministic live probe, and
mutation-oriented tests. The D4/D5 compatibility suite passed 19/19 selected
tests; the documentation suite passed 75/75; and the local commit gate passed
its formatting, lint, audits, freshness checks, and sub-floor tests.

The fixed-roster measurement is still ignored pending the sanctioned campaign
boundary, and no census was run. D5 therefore delivers a trustworthy way to
ask whether a city-like apex exists. The answer itself belongs to the next
measurement, not to an invented label.
