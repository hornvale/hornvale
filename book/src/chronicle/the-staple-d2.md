# The Staple D2

*A staple is not only what a place grows. It is also what can cross the short
distance between two places when one has what the other lacks. The first
exchange in Hornvale is deliberately small: two kinds of subsistence, one
fixed reciprocal bundle, and no promise that a future harvest can pay a debt.*

The Hidage asked whether a settlement could be made large by wiring its
catchment into growth. Every catchment cleared the hamlet ceiling, so that
rung was struck as a uniform rescale. D2 began at the more basic separation:
people are not food, and food is not one undifferentiated number.

## Two stocks, not one

The bake now carries two typed subsistence stocks, A and B. They are fungible
within their own types and never interchangeable. Existing production is
split deterministically by the existing climate/terrain signal, conserving
the total exactly; no new random draw enters the history stream. Existing
`stores` remains accumulated non-edible wealth and is never eaten.

Each person-phase requires a complementary A/B basket. Stock carries forward
without loss. The first version has no currency, barter instrument, debt,
interest, labor promise, conversion rate, price discovery, transport loss, or
storage limit. Those are future typed claims, not hidden assumptions in this
rung.

## Exchange at the local edge

After production, a community reserves its own projected basket and offers
only its surplus to an explicitly reachable one-hop counterparty. Clearing is
simultaneous and deterministic, with a fixed 1:1 reciprocal bundle and pro-rata
allocation under scarcity. A funded chain may pass stock through already
declared exchanges; an unfunded cycle cannot manufacture it. Every attempt is
reported as proposed, accepted, settled, partial, refused, or impossible, but
only delivered stock changes the inventories.

The order matters. Production precedes clearing; clearing precedes typed
consumption; all consumption precedes pressure and growth; existing tribute
comes afterward. A full basket is the exact identity of the old pressure path.
Shortfall is a bounded monotone adapter, so exchange can matter without
silently retuning the old `ASSESS_RATE = GROWTH_RATE/8` calibration.

## The first answer

The authorized replacement probe ran once over same-seed control/treatment
pairs for seeds 1 through 200. The disabled treatment was byte-identical to
its control in all 200 worlds, and typed conservation had no nonzero residual
in any treatment world. The enabled treatment settled exchange in 178 worlds.
It made 3,239,066 attempts: 3,239,066 proposed, 163,146 accepted, 81,573
settled, 81,573 partial, 1,905,072 refused, and 1,170,848 impossible.

The verdict was the one the probe was frozen to be able to return: D2
activates, and it does not cross the instability pole. Treatment-only breaches
were 6/200 for the settlement-count band, 1/200 for collapse share, and 0/200
for alive-at-now. The bars are existing guardrails, not new targets, and the
pole is strictly more than half the world denominator.

One earlier run is kept in the record because deleting a wrong measurement is
not the same as correcting it. Its disabled arm passed a doubled-pressure
factor, so its demographic comparison was invalid. It is not folded into the
verdict; the corrected run is the sole authority.

## What D2 owns, and what it leaves

D2 now owns the first measured climate-to-city link: climate shapes local
production; production shapes typed stock; stock determines exchange access
and dependence; dependence reaches settlement stability through shortfall.
This is a bridge, not a city. The catchment growth ceiling and the open
`SETTLERS_PER_CAPACITY` question remain for later rungs.

The exchange outcomes are a derived study trace. Whether aggregate exchange
metrics belong in the larger census is deliberately open until their query
durability and frequency justify the durable surface. The simulation has
gained a bilateral edge; it has not yet gained a financial system.
