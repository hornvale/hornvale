# The Grammar

*A grammar does not list every sentence a language can say. It names a small
set of operations and the rules by which they compose. This campaign gives
reproduction the same treatment: not a catalogue of species exceptions, but
a vocabulary from which ordinary and fantastic life can be built.*

## Nine verbs instead of a bestiary

Hornvale had life history, population pressure and species traits, but no
public account of how a kind reproduces. Adding a field called `sex` or a
label called `mating system` would have answered too little and implied too
much. It would describe a familiar mammal while leaving spores, budding,
broods, host conversion, manufactured bodies, sequential reproductive roles
and non-reproducing kinds as exceptions.

The reproductive substrate now begins with nine causal operations:

```text
make  join  grow  support  release  copy  change  build  convert
```

A body exposes only the affordances needed to compose those operations:
development sites, support modes, reproductive roles and natural transition
capabilities. Detailed anatomy remains private. A pairborn body can compose
`make + join + grow + support + release`; a budded body can compose `copy +
grow + support + release`; a forged body can begin with `build`; a guestborn
body can require a host through `convert`. An empty affordance set is valid,
so a non-reproducing kind is not a malformed ordinary animal.

The evaluator is pure and ordered. It receives an explicit context — roles,
sites, support, timing, environment, resources and assistance — and returns
the pathways that context permits. It performs no draw and names no species.
The same machinery therefore reaches all of the campaign's probe families
without a branch for any one of them.

## Three truths that may not collapse

The campaign's most important separation is smaller than the operation list:

```text
possibility  what this body and context permit
typicality   how often an allowed outcome occurs
realization  what happened to a cohort or person
```

The species grammar owns possibility. Plain, caller-authored distributions
carry typicality into demography. Realization remains future work at the
population layer, where seeded draws and cohort history belong. A rare event
cannot become impossible because it was rare, and one realized exception
cannot rewrite the distribution it was drawn from.

The demographic handoff makes that distinction executable. It carries
maturity and generation timing, offspring and survival distributions,
dependency duration, care burden, role availability, hybrid outcomes and
replacement balance through pure summaries. Missing measurements are not
silently read as zero, while an explicitly weighted zero remains a real
measurement. Every derived scalar is checked before it crosses the boundary;
finite inputs that overflow during a mean or persistence calculation are
refused rather than handed downstream as infinity.

## A hybrid is a relation

Compatibility is not a species-name pair and not a declaration of shared
genus. It is evaluated in both parental directions from body operations,
development constraints, inherited-material evidence and available
assistance. The two directions may disagree. Outcomes distinguish fertile,
sterile-but-viable, unstable, assisted, magic-only and impossible cases,
without assigning any of them a social meaning.

The final review found a boundary hidden inside that rule. Ordinary
reproduction and hybrid applicability are independent: a kind may reproduce
without any hybrid partner having been queried. A first attempt to distinguish
missing measurements from zero required a hybrid distribution whenever an
ordinary pathway existed, and thereby rejected that common case. The shipped
contract carries hybrid applicability separately, so each distribution is
required only when its own question applies.

## The handoff, not the household

The composition root now translates species affordances and caller-authored
population configuration into a species-independent reproductive substrate.
Neither domain imports its sibling. The adapter is additive and opt-in: no
current world build invokes it, and existing settlement, history and generated
world outputs remain unchanged.

That substrate is the boundary this campaign promised the social successor,
not the social simulation itself. The successor receives reproductive roles,
offspring pathways, dependency and care topology, descent and compatibility
relations, and transition history, then combines them with subsistence,
property, mobility, population pressure, authority, religion and contact. From
those inputs it may later derive recognition, households, partnership,
inheritance, gender arrangements, institutions and their history.

None of those social outcomes shipped here. In particular, biology supplies
affordances; it does not supply destiny. No household, prejudice, hierarchy,
gender identity or social classification crosses the reproductive handoff as a
default.

## Eight probes, no new canon

Pairborn, Turning, Broodweave, Budded, Forged, Guestborn, Crossing and a
non-reproducing control freeze the grammar's structural range. They are test
fixtures, deliberately absent from the canonical species registry. No existing
Hornvale people acquired a reproductive profile merely to make the panel pass.

That restraint defines what The Grammar closes. Reproduction now has a
composable, test-backed substrate and an inert composition-root seam. The
social layer has a stable, test-backed successor contract. Authored
reproductive canon, population realization, full households and institutions,
and future magical transitions remain later campaigns — now later work with a
boundary to build against, rather than exceptions waiting to be invented.
