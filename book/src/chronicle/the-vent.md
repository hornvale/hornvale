# The Vent

The Vent is Hornvale's Waterworld pressure test. It keeps the worldly terrain
and climate projection intact, then adds a compact marine overlay whose inputs
are seabed and water-column substrate, light, pressure, temperature, salinity,
chemistry, currents, and sparse vent sources.

The first slice confirms the contract survives with realm-specific nouns. Four
aggregate stocks are aligned with substrate; current-bearing samples are
ordered and bounded; ordinary observation reports consequences while
diagnostic observation names fields, provenance, and uncertainty. No species,
metabolism, reproductive system, organism population, or universal habitat
wrapper was added.

The result is deliberately incomplete. Vent succession, reef fragmentation,
full current networks, signal distortion, and sea peoples remain follow-ups.
