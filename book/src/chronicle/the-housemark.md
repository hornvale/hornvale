# The Housemark

*A housemark is the sign by which work can be recognized as belonging to a
maker. This one is not painted above the door. It is the relation between the
door, the seat, the screen and what is offered to a guest.*

The Hearth built a grammar for interiors and predicted its cultural use. The
Blocking made that grammar visible and walkable. Yet every occupied dwelling
still drew from the same vocabulary in the same way, because the vessel's
brief carried the people living there and no chamber read them. The world knew
who lived in a house and the house did not.

This campaign joins those halves with one small derived signature. It does not
give fifteen peoples fifteen authored styles. It reads two independent axes
from the society vector already attached to the living occupation:

- hierarchic or communal sociality becomes a command seat or a common bench;
- the width of the in-group becomes an inward, plain or outward threshold,
  expressed by a screen, no extra threshold mark, or water offered to a guest.

The cross makes six housemarks. Every mark lives in the threshold chamber,
because the threshold is the only chamber even a one-room structure must have.
All six are admitted through the one shared pattern inventory and composed by
the same validator as the universal ground, threshold and climate-dependent
hearth. Culture selects relations; it does not select a prefab room.

## A refusal is part of the reading

The scalar for in-group radius is not silently rounded to the nearest style.
Three bands admit the fifteen current peoples, while the gaps between those
bands refuse. A future society authored into a gap must therefore reopen the
policy instead of inheriting an accidental classification. `status_basis` is
left unread: a plausible-sounding mapping from knowledge to a loom or
generosity to a jar would be a stereotype, not a spatial consequence.

An unoccupied or synthetic brief likewise carries no housemark. The manikin is
the identity element for consumers that genuinely need a society fallback; it
is not an invisible tenant whose architecture may be assigned to a ruin.

## The proof found the wrong neighbour

The acceptance test was preregistered over five worlds: recover the housemark
from the composed threshold relations of every living dwelling, without using
the people's name or the prose. Its first run did not merely fail to recognize
some houses. It found that 531 of 1,275 living occupation records reversed a
packed settlement-room address to a neighbouring icosphere vertex. The
cube-sphere room mesh and the history mesh are not inverses. The old lookup
therefore produced 222 rooms with no people and 41 with the wrong people; 16
more records were legitimate surface-and-subterranean occupations sharing one
player-addressable room.

The repair removes the reverse journey. World context now reduces settlement
names and living occupations together into maps keyed by the exact production
room address. When two occupations share that address, the same deterministic
first-settlement order chooses both the name and the people, so the two cannot
diverge.

Run again over seeds 42, 13, 7, 1 and 100, the test accounted for 1,259
distinct built rooms: 1,259 inhabited, none unoccupied, and 16 later collisions.
It recovered 1,259 of 1,259 housemarks, covering all fifteen peoples and all six
classes. The decoder composes each of the six candidates independently and
requires exactly one to match the observed kinds and topological relations. A
mutation making the screen universal collapses the inward/plain distinction
and makes the proof fail.

## A reading can still have an accession cost

The housemark itself is pure, seedless and unserialized. Chamber composition
commits no fact and consumes no stream. But the common bench needed a real
registered concept, and every saved world serializes its concept registry.
That additive `bench` entry moved the seed-42 world golden, so Nathan approved
concept accession epoch 20 and the world and language-root artifacts were
re-pinned. This is not a derivation-stream epoch; it is the cost of adding a
new named thing to a world that records what things it knows.

The rendered consequences are correspondingly small and visible. Existing
chamber transcripts gain the appropriate bench, command seat, screen or guest
water. The locale-band selection remains unchanged, and no ledger fact,
census, laboratory golden or stream roster moved.

## What remains outside the door

This is the living occupant's housemark, not the building's biography. A ruin
does not yet remember its builder; a conquered or hybrid household has no
durable stratum from which to read inherited work; and a people's society
vector does not change through history. Those are later questions about
construction, occupation strata and cultural evolution.

The Staple's R1 is shipped. R2 — why a building has its form — and R3 — how a
city gains districts — remain open, as does the dynamics wheel that must make
cities and ruins exist. A bench has no sitting affordance merely because it
has entered the grammar; sitting belongs to the object-ontology and
verb-as-reaction work already carried by the frontier, not to this reading.
What shipped is narrower: a player can now learn that a dwelling belongs to
its people by looking at how they arrange the threshold.
