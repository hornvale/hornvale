# The Staple D4

*Before a place can become known for what it makes, the world must give it
different things to make and different reasons to want them. D4 asks whether
Hornvale has that gradient yet, without naming a single occupation.*

D3B established that the existing world can expose varied source and
projection regimes, but its first specialization-shaped reading was too easy
to saturate. D4 therefore moved one level earlier. It counted one live
`BakeOccupation` at the existing census denominator, joined it to the same-run
phase-resolved history witness, and required the evidence to survive temporal,
structural, mechanism, and join checks.

## A portfolio is not a function

The diagnostic keeps production, voluntary exchange, imports, shortfall,
coercive transfer, and protection access separate. It retains raw typed values,
normalized composition, phase identity, and declared mechanism availability.
It can say that a profile is seasonal, rotating, transient, or a persistent
candidate; it never turns that reading into `Function`, an occupation, or
persistent social state.

The SugarScape lesson remains the boundary: specialization would need
heterogeneous opportunities, needs, capabilities, movement, and bilateral
exchange to meet in the same local path. A fantasy axis—an anomaly, ruin,
hazard, rare material, or knowledge-gated extraction—may enter later only if
it joins that path as a typed opportunity and consumer. A label or nonzero
value is not a gradient.

## The falsifier's two dead poles

D4 names both ways the reading can fail. The first is no realized
differentiation: no adequately observed communities occupy materially
distinct portfolio regimes. The second is projection or measurement collapse:
opportunities vary, but every community receives the same ranking after
saturation review. Isolated, hub-dominated, single-type, zero-phase,
missing-join, unavailable-mechanism, and coercion-only evidence cannot clear
either pole.

The implementation also closes three measurement traps. A repeated seasonal
cycle may end in a matching partial prefix, but it still needs two complete
periods. Live structural observations are absent rather than silently defaulted
and therefore report access and capability debt. Optional channels remain
attached to their original observation beside the numeric projection, so
unavailable is not confused with observed zero.

The local evidence is intentionally a boundary result: D4's library tests
passed 11/11, its focused D2/D3B/D4 suite passed 36/36, and the local commit
gate passed all four sub-floor chunks. The canonical stage gate also passed
all stage phases. No census was run and no specialization claim was made.

## What the world still owes

The live path currently lacks phase-resolved source/access observations and
structural controls, so the diagnostic reports explicit axis debt rather than
fabricating opportunity values. D4 has established the instrument and its
dead-pole discipline; it has not established that the current settlement world
contains enough usable gradients to derive specialization. Future work must
add or expose a genuine local opportunity consumer first, then rerun this
falsifier before assigning roles.
