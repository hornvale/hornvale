# The Observation Series

This campaign established a deterministic path from an observation manifest to
a checked local package. The CLI validates strict episode records and exports
ordered packets from a declared world; the Atlas preview renders supplied
packet fields; and HV-009, the witnessed stellar-neighborhood candidate,
exported two byte-identical sequences of 900 packets. Firefox rasterized those
packets into `1440×900` frames, and ffmpeg assembled the frames into a
30-second local MP4 with a re-checkable sidecar.

That package is a transport proof, not a finished presentation. Its
Firefox-per-frame process turns a static Atlas layout into film; HV-009 and
its caption remain draft with `approval: null`, and no editorial approval or
seven-package reserve is claimed. There is no custom fullscreen animated,
subtitle-style renderer in this result.

The user approved closing at this technical boundary. The intended fullscreen,
animated, subtitle-style, phone-legible presentation belongs to a new renderer
and presentation campaign, with later editorial review and any production
reserve following from that work.
