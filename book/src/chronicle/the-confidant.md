# The Confidant

[The Hand](./the-hand.md) settled that a possessed body stays awake. Arbitration
runs for it while you ride it, so it keeps its own drives, its own commitment
mode and its own affect, tick after tick — and nothing whatsoever could see
that. The only caller of the accessor holding the host's mode was a test.
Co-presence was real, pinned, and, from inside the world, indistinguishable
from absence.

This campaign gives it a voice. You ask the body you are wearing how it feels,
and it answers, in its own culture's words. **What it says is not what the
arbitration computed, and the difference is the deliverable.**

## Why a truthful host would have shipped nothing

The obvious first version reports the affect arbitration reached, in first
person, and adds the divergence later once the plumbing works.

An inversion pass killed it. The world already has an omniscient affect read:
`needs` reports every co-located creature's felt state *through the same
arbitration that drives it*. A host that reports its arbitration accurately is
that oracle wearing a pronoun — the player learns, by a second route, exactly
what she already knew. The gap is therefore not a refinement layered onto
working machinery. It is the entire reason to prefer testimony over the oracle,
and a version without it has not shipped a smaller thing; it has shipped the
thing that was already there (decision 0256).

So the divergence had to land in the same arc, in two independent kinds. A
creature cannot perceive everything about itself, and its culture cannot name
everything it perceives.

## The cognitive residue was already being computed and discarded

Arbitration ranks every drive and keeps the winner. The ranks it throws away
*are* what a creature cannot introspect: the thirst it is also feeling while it
walks toward warmth is real, computed, and — for the creature — not there.
Nothing had to be invented. The residue had to be named as a stage of the
pipeline and retained beside the winner, so that "what the world knows about
you" and "what you know about yourself" became two objects instead of one
(decision 0258).

It is a list, not a single drive — a body observably suppresses two at once —
and the guard on it is the interesting shape. A test that reddens when the
filter is *deleted* would pass just as happily against a filter that excluded
everything. The one that holds it was proved by **widening**: drop the
exclusion of the drive actually being pursued, and the pursued drive leaks into
the suppressed list, and the test objects.

The lexical residue needed more. For a culture to lack a word for *helpless*,
`helpless` must be a thing a culture can hold a word for — a concept — because
the whole lexical apparatus (exposure, ancestral roots, sound change, gaps and
their recountable reasons) is keyed on the concept registry and nothing else.
So the six felt states of the affect circumplex became registered concepts
under a new kind, acceding as their own generation, appended (decision 0257).

The brainstorm predicted that this would cost a world-generation epoch — new
concepts meaning new word draws, and therefore a changed consumption order,
which is a save-format contract. **Checked before the cohort was designed, and
false.** Concepts sort by their generation first, so an appended cohort lands
strictly last: the one position that provably displaces nothing. The campaign
reproduced that empirically over the real table across eight seeds, with the
control inside the same test — fold the identical concepts into generation
zero instead, and something moves.

Appending was also the operation that exposed an old over-claim. The
insertion-stability test asserted that *no* existing word moved when a cohort
was appended, which is broader than the algorithm actually guarantees: a
concept sorting after the newcomer legitimately may move, because its search
for an unclaimed form depends on what earlier concepts already took. Nothing
had ever sorted after that cohort before. The new generation is the first thing
that does, and one concept moved at one seed. The invariant was narrowed to
what is true and strengthened where the campaign leans on it, rather than
re-pinned to the new numbers.

## Nobody was allowed to author who lacks what

The instrument this arc exists for makes conceptual deficiency visible and
comparable across the fifteen peoples. That is exactly where it could have
destroyed itself.

If a table says which people lacks which feeling-word, then measuring the
distribution of deficiency recovers the table. It is the same failure as
auditing a generator with the generator. So the deficiency is **derived** —
from three authored psychological scalars that predate the question entirely
(decision 0259). A species that meets a blockage by standing has a word for
*frustrated*, one that flees has a word for *lost*; a slow deliberator names
*content*, a fast one *eager*; a generational planner names *helpless*, an
immediate opportunist *searching*. Nobody chose the resulting distribution,
because nobody was thinking about feelings when those numbers were written.

The distribution that fell out is genuinely uneven, and was confirmed to be
load-bearing by breaking the lookup and watching it collapse: goblins hold none
of the six — authored exactly at the neutral midpoint on all three axes, which
is a real reading and not an omission — snow-elves one, hobgoblins and humans
two, the other eleven three.

Three is also the ceiling, and the honesty about that is part of the shipped
artifact. Each scalar governs a *valence-opposed pair* and awards at most one
pole, so no people can ever name more than half its own feelings, and eleven of
fifteen sit exactly at the bound. A reader meeting "no culture has words for
more than half of what it feels" would take it for a finding about impoverished
minds. It is a property of a three-number mapping — real tongues do have words
for both frustration and hopelessness — and the published table says so in its
own header rather than leaving the correction to prose somewhere else.

## The measurement was in the wrong instrument

The three measures — how much of its inner life a people can name, how many
distinct states collapse onto one word, how far the reported state sits from
the true one — were built as census metrics, one family per people, forty-five
columns.

A thousand-seed run reported all forty-five **constant on every row**.

Constant-across-seeds has two causes and only one is benign, so the mechanism
was checked rather than the number accepted. These measures read the real
per-world vocabulary, then branch on whether the culture *has* the concept —
which is exposure-determined, and exposure derives from authored species
attributes. The word's form varies with the seed; its existence does not. The
columns were correctly seed-invariant.

That is disqualifying twice over, and the second reason is the one worth
carrying. The census pays for these on two thousand world-builds a refresh to
learn what one world already knows — measurable, and roughly three times the
stated per-metric tolerance. But worse: **a constant column cannot detect
drift, which is the census's entire job.** It moves all thousand rows at once
or never. A flat column looks exactly like a well-behaved one; it is the
healthiest-looking thing in the table and it is inert.

The project already held the precedent and the campaign had landed on the wrong
side of it. The evaluative-sentiment window is "a pure, world-invariant
derivation, computable before any seed exists" — and it is a *window*, not a
census metric. These were the same shape in the wrong home. They became a
fifteen-row published table computed once, and the schema bind that had blocked
the whole branch — new columns meaning a changed census schema meaning every
census-reading check failing identically — dissolved with the shape error that
caused it (decision 0260).

## What you actually hear

The verb is `ask`, bare and in-character, gated on the body's state the same
way its other self-reports are. The host answers with its own spoken word — the
real derived form of its own tongue, not a gloss — and when its culture has no
word for what it feels, it reaches for the nearest one it does have and says so.

What lands in your knowledge is the *reported* concept, never the true one.
That is not decoration: a later measure comparing what was said against what
was true compares concepts to concepts, and cannot be fooled by a word it
merely failed to translate. The invariant was proved by breaking it — swap the
reported concept for the true label and the truth leaks into the transcript,
and the guard says so in as many words.

The listener end needed no work at all. Heard knowledge has always been
excluded from the ground-truth check, deliberately and with a note saying so,
which means a false statement could already be held as a belief. Testimony is
the first thing to use that seam for what it was cut for.

Ask a goblin how it feels and it has nothing to say about any of it. That is
not a bug and it is not silence for its own sake — it is a mind whose authored
psychology sits at the neutral point on every axis, in a culture that therefore
never had cause to name a single one of the six things it can be. The world
does not tell you this. You have to notice.
