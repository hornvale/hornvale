# Skyworld Seams

Skyworld can borrow a planet without rebuilding it. Its seeded mobile
habitats sit over the existing land and ocean surface, carrying phenotype,
lineage metadata, aggregate orchard stocks, finite daily trajectory samples,
and separate physical, exchange, influence, and rendered footprints. Ordinary
and diagnostic lenses read that derived state. The seam audit asked which
inputs actually reach those habitats, and what observing them costs.

The existing `skyworld_from` boundary already expresses the ownership:
terrain and climate arrive as typed shared references; the composition root
derives the overlay. No wrapper or cache earned its place. `BiomeExpr` stays
climate-owned, and neither the build ladder nor saved world facts gained a
Skyworld entry. Repeated generation, query, and rendering probes record zero
additional terrain or climate constructions.

## A changed source must reach a named consequence

The first plate-count and ocean-fraction experiments changed several inputs
together. They demonstrate integration, but cannot identify an independent
cause. Test-only overrides at the actual consumption sites now isolate that
question while checking that the terrain, climate, and biome projection stay
unchanged.

A ten-degree temperature perturbation raises the ambient profile by ten
degrees and leaves the territories equal. Halving moisture changes the
profile, cloud-water stock, and distribution. Elevation and storm propensity
affect distribution without setting the independently drawn habitat altitude.
Reversing wind or the freshly derived ocean current moves a fixed territory
while preserving its identity, origin, phenotype, and stocks. The cached
climate current is not the current that propagation reads. An isolated score
probe also establishes the edifice contribution of `0.25`.

## Reuse still has a cost

On the seed-42, 40,962-vertex fixture, an inactive overlay performs 81,924
surface-scan visits and constructs one propagation index. Two active
territories perform 122,886 scan visits. Raising their requested samples from
two to eight raises total samples from four to sixteen, movement candidates
from twelve to eighty-four, and adjacency-pair checks from eight to
thirty-two. Footprint size and expansion radius also govern work; these
measurements do not establish a universal linear bound.

Each 256×128 PNG visits 32,768 pixels, constructs two full surface maps
(81,924 visits), and builds one render index. Planet detail makes 736 overlay
marks; Regional and Habitat each make 2,616 on this fixture. Those last two
PNGs differ in palette, with equal traversal counts. Index internals,
sorting, collection comparisons and copying, corridor membership, text
formatting, and PNG encoding are outside the counters. Zero reconstruction
does not mean zero planet-size work.

The reusable result is a four-layer contract: surface substrate, ambient
fields, habitat derivation, and observation. It preserves realm-specific
currents, vents, cave energy, aether bands, and projection rules rather than
implementing another realm. No organisms, species, lifecycle mutation,
tethering, mutable atmosphere, or census refresh was added by the audit.

The [measurement ledger](https://github.com/hornvale/hornvale/blob/main/docs/superpowers/ledgers/2026-09-09-skyworld-seams.md)
records the complete input matrix, asserted counts, and limits. Its final
implementation correction passed ten unit and thirty-five integration probes;
the public-root and build-depth guards also reject the source forms that
earlier permissive matchers silently skipped.
