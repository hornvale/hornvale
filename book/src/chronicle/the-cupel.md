# The Cupel

*Myth, campaign 7.* An assay of the crossing penalty's derivation: does the
magnitude being *derived from the world* — rather than chosen by an author —
change any belief that a matched constant would not?

## The licence under test

When an account of an event passes from one people to another, the myth engine
widens its remembered imprecision by a crossing penalty,
`span(FINEST) / (1 + edges_between(a, b))` — one finest rung, discounted by how
many raids the two peoples have on record between them. The magnitude is a fact
about the world's history, not a number an author picked, and that is the whole
of its constitutional licence. Decision 0021 forbids the engine to hold an
authored preference between two peoples; a *derived* preference is admissible
precisely because no one authored it. [The Retelling](the-retelling.md) built
the penalty; [The Undertow](the-undertow.md) measured that its ordering came out
*inverted*, and observed — without an arm to test it — that an inverted ordering
is equally consistent with a constant. [The Touchstone](the-touchstone.md) then
built the instrument this campaign needed: a per-holder belief-delta that sees
what the divergence aggregate cannot.

The question left open was whether the derivation earns its licence *in effect*:
if a flat constant, tuned to the same average garble, produced the same beliefs,
then the derivation would be decorative and 0021 would be protecting a
distinction with no difference.

## The substrate answered before the hypothesis was frozen

A pre-registration probe read the derived arm's realized crossings across the
twelve-seed panel before any instrument was built. It found the opposite of the
feared degeneracy: the raid-edge count varies *widely* — eight distinct values,
discounts spanning 0.083 to 0.500, no single edge count holding even a third of
crossings. The derived magnitude is not flat.

But it also found where the variation goes to die. On the least-damage winning
route — the account a community actually keeps — 96% of cross-people holders
cross exactly once, and the penalty a single crossing levies is at most half a
finest rung. Against a precision ladder whose rungs are the world's astronomical
and generational periods — tens to hundreds of days apart — half a rung is far
too small to move a remembered day across a rung boundary. The probe bounded the
fraction of holders a *any* sub-rung penalty change could reach at under 5%. The
derivation's variation is real; the ladder that stores belief is too coarse to
render it.

## The assay

Three transmission arms, identical but for the per-crossing magnitude, each
walked by the traced relaxation and diffed by the belief-delta instrument:
`Free` (no penalty), `Constant` (`span(FINEST) / D`, a single denominator), and
`Derived`. `D` is the matched-mean denominator, `1 / mean(1/(1 + edges))` over
the derived arm's own crossings — so the constant delivers the same average
garble *factor*, and a difference between `Derived` and `Constant` is
attributable to the penalty *varying by people-pair*, not to its average level.
The comparison is a dose-matched placebo: `Constant` is the placebo at the same
dose, `Derived` the active magnitude.

Belief is read on two channels the ladder treats differently. The **remembered
day** is quantized onto a rung; the **winning account** — whose line the story
came down, how many boundaries it crossed — is not. A third quantity, the
continuous accumulated **width**, is the engine's internal bookkeeping and no
one believes it; it is reported as the mechanism, never as the headline.

## The result

Over the cross-people holders of the twelve-seed panel, against a matched
constant `D = 4.7881`:

| channel | derived vs constant | reading |
|---|---|---|
| **remembered day** | **1.34 %** (14 / 1046) | decorative |
| winning account | 0.76 % (8 / 1046) | eight holders |
| internal width | 100 % (1046 / 1046) | the mechanism |

The derivation moves the internal width for *every* cross-people holder and the
*remembered day* for one in seventy-five. A matched constant reproduces the
remembered day for 98.66% of them. The per-world matched-mean estimator, which
removes the residual cross-world level mismatch, reads lower still: 0.86%.

The winning-account channel sharpens the point rather than softening it. Of the
fifteen holders whose winning route crosses at two *different* edge counts — the
population best positioned for the derivation's *shape*, its variation by
people-pair, to change which account a community keeps — **not one moved.** The
eight account changes that occur fall among holders the two magnitudes tip
differently between a crossing route and a descent one: an effect of the
penalty's *level*, which the constant shares in full, not of the edge-count
shape that only the derivation carries. And the level itself is a small effect —
introducing a penalty at all, `Constant` against `Free`, moves the remembered
day for 5.35% of holders; the whole crossing penalty, level and derived shape
together, reaches belief for 6.50%, of which the derivation's distinctive
contribution is the 1.34% above.

The provable-zero floor holds by construction and by mutation: on a descent-only
walk, where no step crosses a people boundary, the two magnitudes produce
bit-identical claims over 2014 holders; deleting the same-people guard drives
that agreement from zero to 0.85, and restoring it returns it to zero.

## What it means

The derivation is behaviourally decorative *at the level of belief*, and not
because the raid history fails to vary — it varies a great deal — but because
the belief ladder quantizes that variation away before it reaches a remembered
day. Its surviving reach into belief is the winning account of eight holders in
a thousand. Decision 0021's licence is real: the magnitude *is* derived, no
author ranked the peoples. But on this substrate the licence protects a
distinction the belief layer can barely render.

This is a statement about the *substrate*, not the mechanism. The crossing
penalty is correctly shaped and correctly derived; what starves it is the
coarseness of what belief can hold and the poverty of what belief is *about*.
The lever for making a people's history bear on what its neighbours come to
believe is therefore not the penalty formula — already varied, already derived —
but three things downstream of it: a **finer memory** than a rung snapped to the
nearest astronomical period; **contestable beliefs** — that a people is
beautiful or ugly, a language lovely or crude, a neighbour clever or stupid —
claims with no true value, which two peoples can hold in genuine and unresolved
conflict where a remembered date cannot differ; and a **linguistic distance**
in transmission, so that a message garbles by how far two dialects have drifted
and not only by whether a people boundary was crossed. The language domain
already computes the raw material for the last of these and nothing consumes it.

The Cupel measured a null and, in measuring it, found where the lever is.
