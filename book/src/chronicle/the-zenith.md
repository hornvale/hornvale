# The Zenith

*The zenith is the point directly overhead. Hornvale once held a sun there
forever, not because a world put it there, but because the first astronomy
provider stipulated it. This campaign removed the stipulation and discovered
that the supposed ladder above it had never been a ladder at all.*

Hornvale's Constitution licensed several providers for one domain when a
higher-fidelity answer refined rather than contradicted a lower one. Astronomy
was the surviving example: a constant sun beside a generated star system. The
constant provider had one observable claim — one maximally salient sun with no
period — and downstream code represented everything it omitted as absence: no
system, no calendar, no day, no year.

That sounds like an expressive capability until the claim is stated precisely.
Its content was **acyclicity**, and generated astronomy already represents it.
A tidally locked world has a real star system and a real year, but no local day;
its sun is aperiodic for the same physical reason that the old provider merely
asserted. The retirement therefore removes no possible sky. Every valid world
now carries a generated system and calendar, while `day_length` remains absent
where tidal locking honestly makes a day undefined.

## The ladder with one rung

The constitutional story named four astronomy tiers: constant sun; day and
night; seasons, moons and calendar; realistic multi-body structure. The latter
three never existed as selectable providers. They shipped as successive
deepening inside one generated sky. The only actual choice was between that
whole system and the original stub.

That distinction matters beyond tidying an API. A refinement elaborates one
truth. A mechanism that contradicts its predecessor changes world identity and
is an epoch. Once the constant sun is gone, the provider-tier licence has no
instance left: within-provider refinement remains, but worlds no longer select
coexisting domain implementations as fidelity settings.

The public shape now says the same thing. World construction accepts sky pins,
not a provider choice. A loaded world must carry its `sky-provider` fact;
absence is a malformed ledger rather than a request for an invisible fallback.
The sky always exposes its system and calendar. The only surviving optionality
is physical, such as a locked calendar's missing day.

## What the constant sun had been measuring

The stipulated and generated seed-42 worlds were not cheap and expensive views
of one subject. They were different worlds. The former produced 16 beliefs and
no calendar-derived facts; the latter produced 145 beliefs and 390
calendar-derived founding-solstice facts. That 16-versus-145 contrast is the
one thing the old provider showed clearly: religion changes when the observable
sky changes.

But one stipulated world is a poor instrument for the question the
Constitution attached to it — what religion develops under eternal noon. The
Laboratory already has the stronger instrument: `census-of-faiths`, ten
thousand worlds, the `pantheon-cyclic-share` metric, and pin sets designed to
compare regimes. No committed study has yet used a non-empty pin set. The next
honest experiment is therefore explicit and separate: add a locked-rotation
population to that census and compare derived eternal-noon worlds, rather than
keep a stub alive as an anecdote.

## The surface of deletion

The registry estimated six production files because it counted handlers of the
constant variant. The real public choice was positional on the composition
root's most-called function: 405 choice sites across 335 world-build call sites.
The migration was staged so the 23 sites that truly requested the constant
provider changed subject first, while the choice still existed; only then did
the repeated generated argument disappear.

The two seed-42 almanacs left behind name the distinction that remains: one
ordinary spinning world and one tidally locked world. The cross-tier test did
not disappear with its source provider either. Its expectations were frozen as
three literals outside production and promoted into a sky-conformance battery.
The result is stricter than before: the oracle no longer asks production code
what production code ought to say.

The campaign retired a provider, a fallback, an option layer, and a doctrine.
What survived is the part that was true: coarse detail may be elaborated inside
one mechanism; acyclicity is a physical regime; and a question about a
population belongs in a census.
