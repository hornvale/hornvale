This is a real clearing on the world of seed 42 — vertex 6659 — where
seventeen lives have passed over the ground. The deepest layer was a
mountain-dwarf steading founded in year 0; the standing layers are a
kuo-toa steading founded in year 1750 and a mountain-dwarf steading
