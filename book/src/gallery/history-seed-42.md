# The Contested Clearing of Seed 42

A site read back out of the ledger by the `history` verb: the stratigraphy
of every people that ever settled one cell, oldest layer deepest, and the
derived flesh — the structures they raised, the residue in the grass
today. Nothing here replays the deep-history bake; it is all a
*present-as-query* over committed occupation facts, with the flesh
(structures, residue) derived on demand and never committed.

This is a real clearing on the world of seed 42 — cell 21953 — and eight
gnoll steadings have risen on it, one settling atop the ruins of the
last, from the year 850 down to the present. Only one of the seven
completed layers was ended by winter. Three were put to flight by
another gnoll band wanting the same defensible ground, and three
more ended the other way about — the occupants won a fight somewhere
else and carried the settlement onto the land they had taken. It is a
people with only itself to fight, and this column is what that looks
like from the ground. The eighth was founded in the year 1950 and
stands yet, fifty years on: some twelve souls, two huts and a
granary, and no ruin yet to read.
