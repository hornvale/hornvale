# The Contested Clearing of Seed 42

A site read back out of the ledger by the `history` verb: the stratigraphy
of every people that ever settled one cell, oldest layer deepest, and the
derived flesh — the structures they raised, the residue in the grass
today. Nothing here replays the deep-history bake; it is all a
*present-as-query* over committed occupation facts, with the flesh
(structures, residue) derived on demand and never committed.

This is a real clearing on the world of seed 42 — cell 1400 — and twenty
gnoll steadings have risen on it, one settling atop the ruins of the
last, from the year 500 down to the year 1950. The first held the
ground a hundred seventy-five years before its own people turned
conqueror, driving a rival gnoll band off a neighbouring clearing and
carrying the settlement onto that ground instead — two more of the
twenty would go on to do the same. Winter took most of the rest: the
ice crept down the valley, or the seasons shortened past what the
ground could still feed, and the survivors moved on rather than
starve. But eight of the nineteen completed layers ended a different
way — put to flight not by cold but by another gnoll band wanting the
same defensible ground for itself, a people with only itself to fight.
The twentieth was founded in the year 1950 and stands yet, fifty years
on: eleven souls, a hut and a granary, and no ruin yet to read.
