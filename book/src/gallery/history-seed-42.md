# The Abandoned Clearing of Seed 42

A site read back out of the ledger by the `history` verb: the stratigraphy
of every people that ever settled one cell, oldest layer deepest, and the
derived flesh — the structures they raised, the residue in the grass
today. Nothing here replays the deep-history bake; it is all a
*present-as-query* over committed occupation facts, with the flesh
(structures, residue) derived on demand and never committed.

This is a real clearing on the world of seed 42 — cell 28414 — and fourteen
peoples have failed on it. Most of them arrived the same way: fleeing the
ice of the clearing at cell 7169, looking for kinder ground. They kept
finding the same ground. Hobgoblins held it four times, then kobolds ten
times more, from the year 800 to the year 1750 — and what the layers show
is not progress but repetition: every hobgoblin steading arrives
iron-working and every kobold steading arrives classical, neither ever
advancing past its own tier, and every one of the fourteen held the ground
for exactly twenty-five years. Nor does the count improve: eight souls in
the deepest layer, then six, five, four, eight, six, five, four, three,
three, two, four, three, and three in the last. Not one of the fourteen
was taken by force — every single one was ended by the cold, or by ground
that stopped feeding them. What is left in the grass is potsherds, the low
turf-lines of the dwellings, and a worked pot, chipped at the lip.
