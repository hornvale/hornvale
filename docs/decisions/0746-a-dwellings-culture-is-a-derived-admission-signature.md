# 0746. A dwelling's culture is a derived admission signature over one shared inventory

**Status:** Accepted (2026-09-04) · **Decider:** Nathan (G3 / autopilot) ·
**Campaign:** The Housemark · **Relates:**
[0021](0021-no-alignment-axis.md) (no species-indexed character table),
[0069](0069-fine-position-is-never-serialized.md) (the chamber interior is
derived), [0566](0566-a-place-is-a-graph-before-it-is-a-map.md) (the shared
pattern grammar)

In the context of making a dwelling legibly belong to its living people, we
decided that **culture is one small signature derived from authored society
data and used to admit relational patterns from the one existing inventory**,
accepting that several peoples intentionally share a grammar class.

`HousemarkGate` is an additional chamber-selection predicate on `Pattern`, not
a second cultural inventory, composer, validator, or post-composition rewrite.
Universal necessities remain universal. The existing screen row becomes the
inward-threshold reading, while command seating, common seating, and guest
water are three chamber-only rows appended to the inventory.

An absent housemark admits only universal patterns. Pattern names remain the
stable selection keys, and the composed anchor relations — not people names or
decorative labels — are the observable signature.
