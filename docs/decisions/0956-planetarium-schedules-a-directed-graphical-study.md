# 0956. Planetarium schedules a directed graphical study

**Status:** Accepted (2026-09-10) · **Decider:** Nathan

In the context of the Observation Series static preview failing to deliver the
intended visual quality, we decided to schedule Planetarium as a native graphical
observation client and individually directed ten-second study, accepting that
visual quality and production evidence must establish a sustainable cadence.

**Context.** Nathan approved the Planetarium design at G3. This narrowly amends
the graphical-client deferral in the long-term spec §7; constitutional simulation
ownership and the query surface remain intact.

**Consequence.** One observable may use multiple scales/shots/layers when they
explain that same idea. No fixed film template is required. The Observation
Series daily cadence, mandatory astronomical release staircase and seven-package
reserve are suspended. The study sits outside the older 30–60-second public
episode definition. Existing manifests and records remain historical; no evidence
or approval is upgraded retroactively. Exact video/copy publication still requires
Nathan's approval. This does not settle a future situated game's presentation
hierarchy.
