# 0887. D2 clears exchange simultaneously

**Status:** Proposed at G3 (2026-09-06) · **Decider:** Nathan review pending ·
**Campaign:** The Staple D2

D2 reserves projected current-phase consumption, allocates scarce offers
simultaneously and pro-rata, and applies deliveries atomically. Funded acyclic
chains may use incoming stock; unfunded cycles cannot settle. Exchange
outcomes are explicit derived trace records and do not create debt.
