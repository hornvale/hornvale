# 0748. Both housemark axes live at the entry threshold

**Status:** Accepted (2026-09-04) · **Decider:** Nathan (G3 / autopilot) ·
**Campaign:** The Housemark · **Relates:**
[0566](0566-a-place-is-a-graph-before-it-is-a-map.md) (the chamber graph),
[0646](0646-the-inhabited-reading-is-a-function-of-the-plan.md) (the
inhabited reading is derived)

Because a legal structure contains from one through `MAX_CHAMBERS` chambers,
we decided that **both cultural axes are expressed in `Role::Threshold`, the
only chamber every dwelling owns**. Structure size may change what lies beyond
the mark, never whether the mark is present.

Command and common authority therefore place their diagnostic seating at the
entry. Inward and outward postures respectively add a screen and guest water
there. `Plain` is an intentional zero on the posture axis; its authority
relation still makes the dwelling legible.

This rejects both requiring a second chamber and repeating the signature in
every chamber. The first would change structure generation, while the second
would turn a mark into wallpaper and invite duplicate kinds.
