# The Murrain retrospective

The campaign's central decision was to separate the population substrate from
the population projection. That was the right boundary. Worldgen now owns the
causal population and connected host population; Lot can remain a statistical,
salience-weighted reader that materializes only what interaction needs.

The most important correction was replacing summed occupation peaks with a
trajectory reconstruction. Peaks are a useful upper bound, but they are not
the population an epidemic sees. The review caught this before it became a
false epidemiological authority. The same review discipline caught the need
for an outbreak-event subject distinct from its location, and the need to
exercise both sides of the persistence threshold rather than only asserting
the catalogue's authored numbers.

The preregistered readout behaved as it should. Four hypotheses passed and two
were falsified. The plague-ending and outbreak-event bounds were not relaxed
after unblinding. That is the useful result: the model produces disease, but
the chosen world scale and spillover regime produce fewer large events than
the initial prediction expected.

The campaign also exposed the cost of changing a deep-history substrate. The
world moved enough to invalidate witnesses in laboratory, worldgen, game, and
client code. Re-pinning those content witnesses was appropriate when the test
specified a readout; the cross-language collision and additive-baseline cases
were not, and stayed structural or required a deliberate baseline decision.
The founder-collision sweep was especially valuable: contention increased from
one seed in 3,000 to three, showing that thinning settlements can concentrate
survivors onto scarcer viable ground.

The practical process lesson is to profile and verify generated surfaces early.
The Lot bundle drift check was present and correctly caught a stale minified
bundle after the source changed. Regenerating it before the new stage request
kept the failure at the local boundary. The canonical queue then tested the
actual merge product, as intended.

Deferred work has explicit homes:

- Move `windows/lot/src/shape.rs` into a history-owned lower layer when another
  reader needs the population reconstruction.
- Add a household contact lattice before claiming within-community mixing is
  resolved.
- Mint the miasma-as-appearance and illness-in-possession ideas when their
  consumers exist; the registry records them rather than leaving them in
  scratch notes.

The campaign did not run the census locally. Canonical census and Gnomon work
went through the sluice, and the stage request passed before closeout. The
remaining merge is therefore a queue operation, not an invitation to bypass
the canonical host.
