# The Vent — retrospective

The Waterworld pressure test preserved the Skyworld pattern without copying
its nouns. Existing terrain and climate remain the substrate; ambient fields
are explicit; stocks are aggregate; currents are sparse movement evidence; and
observation distinguishes ordinary consequences from diagnostic causes.

The strongest evidence is source isolation. Depth changes pressure and light;
insolation changes light; explicit temperature, salinity, chemistry, and
current perturbations reach their corresponding fields. The seeded vent stream
is isolated at `waterworld/vent/v1`, keyed by stable seabed vertex, and consumes
admission before source-strength values in fixed order.

The measured loop is bounded by the generated substrate vector. Counters count
candidate and accepted propagation samples at the actual loop. Seed-42 focused
Waterworld tests pass with finite bounded stocks, ordered current samples, and
deterministic vent identity. The local commit gate passes 383/383 subfloor
tests; generated stream and audit reports were refreshed only when hooks
demonstrated drift.

The controller had to finish the small Stage 2 and Stage 3 slices after
delegated workers repeatedly stopped before editing. That exception is in the
campaign ledger. The implementation remains intentionally small: no 3-D fluid
solver, dense planet-by-time cache, per-microbe simulation, species system, or
universal habitat wrapper.

At close, `origin/main` had advanced beyond the campaign base. The first
merge-tree check exposed generated-report conflicts; the branch absorbed
`origin/main` and regenerated those reports before queue submission. This was
the campaign's first meeting with main after implementation began, so the
stage-boundary absorption cadence was missed and is recorded here rather than
silently treating the queue's merge as a substitute.

Deferred minors were routed as follows: vent succession, reef fragmentation,
full current networks, and signal distortion are registry rows; sea peoples
and named marine consumers are a registry row; the species/metabolism/
reproduction non-goal remains in the chronicle and ledger as a future design
boundary. No deferred minor changes the shipped Waterworld slice.
