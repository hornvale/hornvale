# The Murrain Across Worlds retrospective

This follow-up turned Lefford's committed comparison into durable project
knowledge. The important correction was one of scale: the aggregate change is
only a few percent, while individual worlds can lose or gain most of their
population. Recording both views prevents the mean from disguising the
redistribution.

The follow-up also kept three readings separate: the across-world census
distribution, seed 42's local redistribution between peoples, and the absence
of a committed across-world drow series. The seed-42 drow count is useful
context, but it is not evidence for a drow population distribution. Kobold
settlements were retained only with the small-denominator caveat, including
the misleading upper-tail percentage.

No code or measurement instrument change was justified. The committed census
already measures the aggregate population and settlement quantities needed for
this result. Adding a drow column would be a separate measurement campaign
requiring metric, roster, schema, and canonical census work; substituting
kobold would be a category error. The follow-up therefore ran no census and
preserved the existing timing ledger.
