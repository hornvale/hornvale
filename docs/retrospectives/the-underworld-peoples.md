# The Underworld Peoples — retrospective

**Closed:** 2026-09-10

This campaign's main process lesson is that a census delivery is not a
campaign close. The goldens were successfully regenerated first, but the
delivery branch was held because the chronicle, retrospective, and
reconciliation record were still missing. The queue caught that omission
before `main` moved.

## What worked

Measurement preceded authoring. The admission probe established reachable
rungs, wet/dry coverage, and overlap before the four requested kinds entered
the registries. Keeping species, environment, seating, and language as
separate projections prevented a roster expansion from becoming an allocator
rewrite. The test suite also kept the aquatic medium distinct from the
subterranean realm.

The final merge with `main` exposed one ordinary generated-artifact conflict in
`docs/audits/plumb-roster.md`. Resolving it by rerunning `tools/plumb -- report`
was safer than choosing a stale count by hand, and the subsequent commit gate
passed all four sub-floor chunks.

The naming optimization stayed appropriately local. A per-run cache removed
duplicate autonym resolution without changing ordering, collision behavior, or
the deterministic stream contract. The census improved from 1986 seconds to
1913 seconds on the comparable queue runs.

## What remains open

The latest census body took 1434.633 seconds and crossed the yellow threshold.
The required follow-up records the CPU increase and the absence of a live
flamegraph from this Mac; attribution remains open for a quiet canonical-host
profile. The campaign did not alter the alarm or claim that the optimization
explained the entire census.

The campaign's scratch directory contained an orphaned Task 1 patch and brief,
plus the plugin progress ledger. The patch and brief describe the already
committed probe and have no independent deferred work. The durable campaign
ledger contains three G-stage entries; it has no deferred-minor entries. The
only carried-forward item is the profiling follow-up, recorded in
`docs/timings/census-yellow-log.tsv`.

No existing frontier row was authored specifically by this campaign. The
vertical-relationship bet remains raw: this campaign supplied people and
medium-sensitive seating, but not the deeper surface–underground mechanisms
that would resolve it. The Confidence Gradient is therefore re-scored as
partial evidence, not as a shipped resolution.
