# The Living Vent — retrospective

The Living Vent extended The Vent at its existing composition-root seam. The
important architectural choice was to keep `WaterWorld` stable and derive a
pure `WaterWorldSnapshot` at exact world time. That made succession a
deterministic read rather than a mutable history, cache, or event queue.

Source-first probes were the campaign's strongest discipline. A temporal red
first proved that climate temperature really changed before the overlay was
allowed to claim change. Later probes perturbed actual vent source fields,
terrain nutrients, and current vectors, then asserted downstream differences
while freezing unrelated substrate, identity, and fields. This caught a
vertex-only phase offset, a local-input-only memory probe, global-current
transport, and an uncounted diagnostic stock traversal before close.

The chosen state cycle is intentionally categorical and bounded. The memory
probe showed that present nutrient and reef/kelp values already decline from
active through weakening to failed, so no residue or temporal stock cache was
earned. A four-neighbour candidate ring and three-hop current propagation
provided migration and transport evidence at bounded work. Counters were
placed in the actual refresh, source, stock, propagation, and observation
loops; the seed-42 snapshot measured 83,997 refresh and stock rows and 7,038
propagation candidates.

The campaign's process cost was mostly coordination and canonical validation,
not implementation. The local gate passed with a final 58.028-second run;
the Sluice stage gate passed all four phases in 1,483.5 seconds on its real
merge product. The branch had to absorb the current `origin/main` after that
stage report before close; the merge was clean and required the repository's
normal generated-artifact rebaseline. That first post-stage absorption is
recorded here because the branch's earlier stage-boundary cadence was missed.

Scratch review found no unpromoted material finding. The ledger's deferred
minors route as follows: full current networks remain `WAT-reef-fragmentation`
and the future current follow-up; signal distortion remains
`WAT-signal-distortion`; sea peoples and named marine consumers remain
`WAT-sea-peoples`; and species, metabolism, reproduction, and per-organism
ecology remain explicit non-goals in The Vent and The Living Vent. The
registry row for vent succession now points here as shipped.

The durable lesson is that temporal evolution is explainable when present
consequences and inferred causes are separate products. Ordinary output says
what the water is doing; diagnostic output says which bounded derived phase
may explain it and marks that explanation as uncertain.
