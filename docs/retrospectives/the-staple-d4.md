# The Staple D4 — retrospective

*The chronicle carries the measurement boundary. This record carries the
process lessons and the work that must not be lost when the worktree is
recycled.*

## Start one level earlier than the requested feature

The campaign began as specialization design and became a gradient-sufficiency
test. That was the right inversion. D3's uniform result did not prove that
specialization was impossible; it could have been a saturated instrument or a
world with too little variation. SugarScape supplied the useful comparison:
heterogeneous resources, needs, capabilities, movement, and local exchange
create the pressures from which roles may emerge. D4 therefore refused to
author roles before proving that those pressures exist.

## The measurement needed several anti-vacuity passes

The first implementation review found four risks: repeated seasonal cycles
were too narrowly recognized, structural vacuity was not represented, orphan
source rows could disappear, and optional channels were recoverable only from a
second report field. The corrective cycle made each branch explicit. In
particular, a partial repeated cycle is admissible only after two complete
periods; a live build with no structural observation carries debt; and each
reported phase keeps its original optional observation alongside its numeric
profile.

The lesson is broader than D4: a diagnostic can be mathematically correct and
still be operationally vacuous if its unavailable inputs are defaulted into
plausible values. Missingness must travel with the observation until the final
verdict.

## Scratch and ledger routing

The post-G3 ledger entries are durable in
`docs/superpowers/ledgers/2026-09-08-the-staple-d3b.md`, including the final
resolution in entry #43. The D4 scratch directory contained the plugin
progress record, task briefs/reports, and review diffs. Their substantive
findings are represented in the committed ledger and this retrospective; the
review diffs are not product artifacts and have no separate durable home.

No deferred minor from D4 is silently carried. The absence of live
source/access observations and structural controls is recorded as axis debt in
the implementation and remains the next measurement prerequisite. Fantasy
axes—anomalies, ruins, hazards, rare materials, and knowledge-gated
extraction—remain deferred until they join a typed settlement consumer and
survive the D4 falsifier. Persistent specialization, learning, movement,
prices, and `Function` labels remain explicitly out of scope.

## Close evidence

D4 library tests passed 11/11; the focused D2/D3B/D4 suite passed 36/36; the
local commit gate passed all four sub-floor chunks; the canonical stage gate
passed all stage phases; and the canonical merge gate passed all merge phases,

campaign therefore closes as a diagnostic instrument and a conservative
negative/underpowered boundary, not as a specialization implementation.
