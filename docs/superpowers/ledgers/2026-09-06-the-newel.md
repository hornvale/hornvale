### R1 — B3 is real, and the mechanism is a disagreement between two
### correct components

`windows/vessel/examples/rose_probe.rs`, `rose_edge.rs`, `rose_ids.rs`
(throwaway).

- The walk-band chart is **north-up and placed by true bearing**:
  `scene/surrounds/v2` declares `orientation: "north-up"`
  (`windows/scene/src/surrounds.rs:424-437`) and the client projects each
  cell from its `bearing_deg`/`distance_rad` polar pair —
  `row = round(-cos(bearing) * r)`, `col = round(sin(bearing) * r * 2)`
  (`clients/game/core/src/chart.rs:19-21`, `:184`).
- A compass WORD, by contrast, is assigned by
  `hornvale_locale::heading_rose` (`windows/locale/src/lib.rs:2160`), a
  lexicographic min-max one-to-one assignment of the eight words onto the
  facet's actual neighbours. Its worst-case error is pinned at
  `ROSE_WORST_DEG = 34.578` degrees (`windows/locale/src/lib.rs:3529`).
- 34.578 > 22.5, so **the word and the drawn sector can disagree**, and do.
  Measured over a uniform 6,144-facet sample at walk depth 13:

  | word | pairs | drawn in a different 45-degree sector | pct |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | N | 6143 | 92 | 1.50% |
  | NE | 6144 | 194 | 3.16% |
  | E | 6140 | 310 | 5.05% |
  | SE | 6144 | 234 | 3.81% |
  | S | 6143 | 124 | 2.02% |
  | SW | 6144 | 234 | 3.81% |
  | **W** | **6144** | **278** | **4.52%** |
  | NW | 6144 | 194 | 3.16% |
  | ALL | 49146 | 1660 | 3.38% |

- It is **not** a seam-only effect, which is the finding that makes the
  report credible for ordinary play. By Chebyshev distance from the nearest
  face edge:

  | edge distance (facets) | pairs | misdrawn | pct |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0 | 31272 | 5848 | 18.70% |
  | <= 8 | 115200 | 15040 | 13.06% |
  | <= 64 | 480528 | 55528 | 11.56% |
  | <= 1024 | 109056 | 5240 | 4.80% |
  | > 1024 | 190512 | 2848 | 1.49% |

- A 24-step `go west` walk from seed 42's flagship (face 1, face-lattice
  `(7214, 3655)`, depth 13) moved `(-1, 0)` in the face lattice every time
  at a constant 2.7 degrees of error — so a straight walk in one region
  shows nothing, and the effect appears when the local rose is rotated.
  This is why the report says *sometimes*.
- Neither component is wrong on its own.

**BUT THE SECTOR STORY ABOVE IS NOT WHAT NATHAN SAW, AND THE REAL ONE IS
SIMPLER AND WORSE.** Two corrections, both measured:

1. **`chart::draw` is dead code in the shipped client.** `spread::compose`
   (`clients/game/core/src/spread.rs:255-261`) calls it only when
   `world_plate` is `None`, and `world_plate_for_redraw`
   (`driver.rs:1407`) returns `Some` exactly when `raster_is_drawn()` —
   which is `self.on_walk_band` (`driver.rs:1883-1885`), set from
   `matches!(snap.spatial, Spatial::Walk{..})` (`driver.rs:2925`). The two
   conditions are the same condition, so on the walk band the raster always
   wins and the chart is never drawn. Any analysis of the chart's
   projection is off the path a player drives.
2. **The surface that IS drawn reproduces the report at the flagship's own
   starting room, with no seam and no polar face.** The world plate is a
   north-up Mercator raster; the observer's mark is pinned at plate centre
   (decision 0292, centre on arrival) and the WINDOW scrolls under it.
   Forty `west` steps from seed 42's flagship (throwaway
   `clients/game/bin/examples/walk_marker.rs`):

   ```
     n      latitude     longitude    mark(x,y)      d lat      d lon
     1     -4.003281   -145.750120        20,10  -0.000521  -0.010980 win(3097,16685)
    ...
    18     -4.012116   -145.936890        20,10  -0.000519  -0.010990 win(3080,16685)
    19     -4.012635   -145.947880        20,10  -0.000518  -0.010990 win(3079,16686)
         ^^^^ WINDOW MOVED (-1,+1) -- a DIAGONAL scroll
    ...
    40     -4.023486   -146.178590        20,10  -0.000515  -0.010990 win(3058,16687)
         ^^^^ WINDOW MOVED (-1,+1) -- a DIAGONAL scroll
   ```

   Thirty-eight of forty presses scroll the map `(-1, 0)`, pure west. Two
   of forty scroll `(-1, +1)` — **west and south together, which on screen
   is a step to the south-west.** That is the report, verbatim, at a rate
   of about one press in twenty.

**The cause needs no assignment error at all.** This room's `west`
neighbour bears **267.28 degrees**, not 270 — the closest of the eight the
lattice offers. `cos(267.28 deg) = -0.047`, so every west step carries
0.047 of a plate row southward; after ~21 steps that is a whole row and
the raster scrolls diagonally. The 34.578-degree worst case makes it
happen every other step; **2.7 degrees is already enough to make it
happen, and 2.7 degrees is the best this lattice can do here.**

**So B3 is not a defect in any component.** The movement is right (the
nearest neighbour to west), the drawing is right (the mark is at the
body's true position), and the player's model — "west means straight left
on a north-up map" — is the thing that cannot hold: a cube-sphere lattice
row is not a parallel of latitude, and eight discrete neighbours cannot
be at exact 45-degree intervals. Nothing in the client says so. **The
campaign's real question for B3 is what the client should DISCLOSE, not
what it should compute.**

Note one option is closed before it is raised: a held course or rhumb
would make a west walk self-correcting, and The Pavement deleted
`crate::course` deliberately (`session.rs:7338-7360`: "There is no
reckoned point, no held course and no rhumb"). Reintroducing it reverses a
ratified design and is a G3 flagged item, not a free choice.

### R4 — RETRACTED, and the retraction is the finding

An earlier revision of this ledger recorded that
`clients/game/bin/src/plate.rs:661` `OCEAN_COLOR` and `:663`
`SALT_BASIN_COLOR` were dead constants, on the strength of a `cargo build`
warning. **They are not dead**: both are read by `plate::color_for`
(`plate.rs:775-776`), verified by grep in a quiescent tree.

The warning was real and the reading of it was wrong. A reconnaissance
subagent was holding a live mutation of `color_for` (its body replaced
with a constant colour, to prove the function is on the player's path) at
the moment the build ran; with `color_for` stubbed, both constants
genuinely are unused. The tell was in the build output and was read
past: `Blocking waiting for file lock on build directory`.

**The lesson, which is general:** a build or test run while a subagent
holds a mutation reports the SUBAGENT's tree, not yours. Parallel
reconnaissance and a shared worktree do not mix for anything that
compiles. Either serialise the compiling work or give each probing agent
its own worktree.

### R5 — B5 is real, and the written confession is a red herring

- **`plate.rs:938-940`'s comment is about MARKERS, not terrain.** It sits
  on `MARK_COLOR`, a const consumed only by
  `plate::draw_perception_layer` (`plate.rs:2005-2010`), and points at
  `HERE_COLOR` (`plate.rs:931-936`), whose doc explains that tinting the
  observer's own marker with the wire's surface colour would say the
  marker was ground. That is a correct, deliberate choice. Starting from
  it, as the campaign brief suggested, leads away from the bug.
- **The real defect is inside `reflectance_at_facet`.** The facet is
  addressed per-facet, but two of the three dominant colour drivers are
  read at ONE vertex: `windows/locale/src/lib.rs:979-993` takes
  `dominant_corner(weights)` for `biome_expr_at`, and `:1050-1058` takes
  it again for `material_at` and `rock_at`. Only moisture is blended, plus
  a small per-facet `micro` term.
- **`water` and `ocean` are strictly per-vertex** —
  `plate.rs:2775` `terrain.water_kind_at(vertex)` and `:2847`
  `terrain.is_ocean(vertex)` — and `color_for` (`plate.rs:769-779`)
  branches on `tile.water` BEFORE it ever reaches reflectance. So the
  coastline drawn at rung 13 is a 110 km Voronoi edge between icosphere
  vertices, not a coastline.
- Measured (throwaway `newel_recon2.rs`), 200x60 window, equatorial:

  | rung | tiles | distinct facets | distinct vertices | distinct colours |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 6 | 12000 | 11284 | 9506 | 673 |
  | 10 | 12000 | 12000 | 76 | 18 |
  | **13 (shipped default)** | **12000** | **12000** | **5** | **1** |

  `colours / vertex` is ~1.0-1.5 at every rung: the colour field's
  resolution IS the vertex field's resolution.
- **Proved on the player's path** two ways: the shipped call
  `Driver::world_plate_for_redraw` (the one `main.rs:285` makes) renders
  at rung 13 with 9 distinct inks; and mutating `color_for` to a constant
  changed 1312 of 1352 drawn boxes. Mutating the reflectance address to
  the grid-level ancestor collapsed the six land greys to one — so the
  per-facet term contributes about an 11% luminance jitter on a colour
  otherwise fixed by the vertex quad.
- **Nothing pins the rendered plate byte-for-byte.** No golden, no
  committed render. Every pin is a property assertion — palette count > 9
  (`bin/tests/wash.rs:679`), a chromaticity floor (`:727`), a bare
  majority of `color_for`'s colours reaching the plate (`:742`), and
  `assert_eq!(terrain.is_ocean(tile.vertex), tile.ocean)`
  (`bin/tests/plate_vocabulary.rs:153-157`), **which pins the vertex-level
  water read and will have to move with the fix.**
- Decisions that REQUIRE the fix: **0687** (a derived surface reads
  continuous causes, never categorical labels — and it names this exact
  failure: "a per-vertex categorical answer read across a 1.126 km walk
  band paints a uniform label across the whole band"), **0121** (a nominal
  field takes a partition, evaluated per room), **0667** (the ratified
  vertex-to-facet precedent, re-siting a placed site by seeded draw).
  Decisions that CONSTRAIN it: **0676** (a view may interpolate between
  samples, never invent below them — a refined reading stays inside the
  convex hull of its samples), **0389** (colour carries category and
  substance, and colour may fail), **0716/0718** (the sim emits spectra,
  the view owns the observer; appearance is derived, never committed).
  Schema freedom: **0356** retired the external clients, so a
  `scene/surrounds` change is no longer a cross-repo contract — but
  **0513** requires `SurroundsCell.w`/`up` stay null.
- One more structural fact worth carrying: **the wire's per-facet colour
  has no reader in the shipped TUI.** `core/src/chart.rs:325` `draw`
  consumes `cell.color`, but `spread::compose` calls it only when
  `world_plate` is `None`, and on the walk band the raster always wins
  (`driver.rs:1883-1885`). So `chart::draw` survives for tests and
  library callers only.

### R6 — B2 is a DIFFERENT BUG than the one reported

The report asks for `enter Doaba` to work. It already does — and so does
`enter banana`.

- **Outdoors, `enter` never reads its argument.** `fn enter`
  (`windows/vessel/src/session.rs:7457`) has two disjoint branches; the
  out-of-doors one (`:7550-7595`) does not mention `target` at all,
  verified mechanically:
  `awk 'NR>=7550 && NR<=7596 && /target/' windows/vessel/src/session.rs`
  prints nothing. It resolves the facet's single site through
  `structure_at`, gated on `brief.site` (decision 0666: at most one
  enterable site per facet), so there is nothing to disambiguate BY
  CONSTRUCTION and the argument is dead weight.
- RUN, seed 42, script-driven `possess`, standing on Doaba's facet:

  ```
  > enter Doaba     -> [chamber — threshold] A small room in Doaba, ...
  > enter banana    -> [chamber — threshold] A small room in Doaba, ...
  > enter Nenotata  -> [chamber — threshold] A small room in Doaba, ...
  ```

  `Nenotata` is a real settlement on the other side of the planet.
- **This is the one verb in the roster that still swallows an argument**,
  and the file forbids it in its own words elsewhere —
  `session.rs:5649-5652` (`map`'s indoor arm) and `:4022-4028` (`sleep`):
  *"an argument indoors is refused rather than silently ignored — an
  ignored argument is how a player comes to believe they asked for
  something and got it."*
- **Decision 0788 indicts it directly:** *"the footer's own words must be
  typable"*. The walk-band prose prints `You can enter the settlement of
  Doaba.` (`session.rs:7318-7328`) and the parser accepts `Doaba`,

  (`clients/game/bin/src/input.rs:125`); the engine is
  `clients/game/core/src/lexicon.rs` (case-insensitive prefix, unique

  source is `narration.nouns` — one call site,
  `driver.rs:2920 self.scope.update(&snap.narration)`. At the flagship
  that is six entries. So `enter Doab[TAB]` completes and
  `enter Dvoas[TAB]` does not, **even though `examine Dvoashngashngo`
  resolves fine** — the completion set is strictly narrower than the
  resolver's.

  names (`Xo` x25); the `D`-initial roster contains `Doaba`,

  `{"noun":"Doaba","kind":"settlement",...}` for every marked settlement,
  and `Mark`/`LegendEntry` are already in the client-core schema

- Constraining decisions: **0788** (a way is named by role; more than one

  The Ken's refuse-don't-guess rule is code and prose only

### R7 — B6 is real, one line wide, and comes with a second finding

- **The exclusion is `plate::color_for`** (`clients/game/bin/src/plate.rs:769-779`):

  ```rust
  match tile.water {
      0 => obs.show(OCEAN_COLOR),
      1 => obs.show(SALT_BASIN_COLOR),
      _ => obs.observe(tile.reflectance.as_ref()?, illum),
  }
  ```

  `TerminalObserver::show` (`observer.rs:80-88`) takes no illuminant at
  all — it only quantizes an sRGB triple. So `illum` is unused on the two
  wet arms.
- It is **deliberate and documented**, with a stated reason
  (`plate.rs:753-763`): `reflectance_at_facet` integrates the GROUND's
  cover mixture, which is what lies *under* an ocean, and *"there is no
  spectrum in the sim for 'open water seen from orbit'."* So the fix is
  not "delete the special case" — it needs a water-surface spectrum, or a
  defensible client-side stand-in that the illuminant can still act on.
- RUN, seed 42, clean tree at `1f48bb39d`, driving both sun elevation
  directly and real `WorldTime` across a day:

  ```
  elev   80.0  ocean [20, 60, 160]  basin [230, 230, 200]  land [127, 126, 109]
  elev    0.0  ocean [20, 60, 160]  basin [230, 230, 200]  land [ 34,  23,   1]
  elev  -60.0  ocean [20, 60, 160]  basin [230, 230, 200]  land [ 34,  23,   1]
  ```

  Over the whole 80x24 window the ocean ink set is a **singleton at both
  noon and deep night**. 1439 of 1920 tiles in that window are ocean, so
  the world map is 75% frozen.
- **The walk-band chart, by contrast, is correct** —
  `surrounds_scene_colored_in` (`windows/scene/src/surrounds.rs:812`) has
  no water branch and senses every cell under the light. Standing on open
  water, chart colours cycle `[33,33,1]` (night) to `[133,133,120]`
  (noon). **So the map plate's ocean is the only surface in the client
  frozen against the clock.**
- **The player can reach open water: 18 `northeast` steps from the
  flagship.** `go` has no water gate.
- **A SECOND, SEPARATE FINDING — night has no gradation anywhere.**
  `at_elevation` (`domains/astronomy/src/illuminant.rs:41-58`) clamps
  `airmass` at `MAX_AIRMASS = 38.0`, so every sun elevation at or below
  ~1.5 degrees yields the identical illuminant, and no moon or star
  contributes to any illuminant in the repo. Land snaps to one fixed night
  colour and stays there. Not water-specific, but squarely inside the
  report's *"day/night/etc cycle"*.
- **A THIRD, also separate:** the habitat descriptor can read `sunlit` at
  midnight — `windows/locale/src/grammar.rs:218-227` keys the light word
  off the static `micro.aspect`, never off the clock. Observed on land too
  (`buttressed canopy, sun-warmed, in a hollow ... The sky above: Night.`),
  so it is a descriptor-vs-clock bug, not an open-water one.
- **Nothing pins the current behaviour, and one test explicitly excludes
  it.** `wash.rs:701` reads its population from `spectral_land_colours()`,
  which does `if t.water < 2 { continue; }`, and its doc says why:
  collapsing the spectral arm to greyscale left the whole-plate hue
  assertion GREEN, *"satisfied entirely by ocean, salt basin and river."*
  No test anywhere renders the plate at two sun elevations and compares.
- Decisions: **0716** (*a view consumes spectra and owns its observer; the
  sim never emits a colour*) is the record that made the map's old
  elevation-keyed RGB table wrong, and `OCEAN_COLOR` is the surviving
  instance of that pattern; **0717** (a layer may never read
  faster-changing data — the terrain layer is declared `Diurnal` while its
  ocean arm reads nothing diurnal); **0718** (appearance is derived, never
  committed); **0408** forbids a tint carrying an *epistemic* state and
  says nothing against an illuminant carrying physical light — it
  constrains the fix's shape, it is not the cause. **No decision anywhere
  exempts water from the illuminant.**

### R8 — a real defect found in passing, off the player's path

`clients/game/core/src/chart.rs`'s projection is badly under-resolved and
silently drops cells. At seed 42's flagship room, radius 4:

```
cells drawn: 53 of 81
boxes holding more than one cell: 28 (of 53 occupied)
```

Three of the eight IMMEDIATE neighbours are not drawn at all, including
the due-south one. The cause is `r = distance_rad / farthest * rings`
with `farthest` the ring-4 DIAGONAL distance: an orthogonal ring-k cell
gets `r ~ 0.707k`, so rings 1 and 2 both round to row 1. Which cell
survives is decided by `box_rank`'s last component — ascending index,
i.e. facet id — so a farther cell routinely displaces a nearer one.

**This is off the shipped path** (see R1's correction 1), so it is a
follow-up rather than a task: it costs a player nothing today, and it will
cost the next campaign that revives the chart everything.

## Open questions carried into the spec

- **B3.** Nothing computes a wrong answer. What should the client
  disclose, and where, so that a diagonal scroll stops reading as a
  malfunction? A held course is closed by The Pavement (see R1).
- **B1.** `>` needs a rule for a facet offering more than one descent. The
  Ken's precedent (an ambiguous noun is refused, not guessed) is the
  obvious candidate and needs to be checked against its decision record.

## Follow-ups

- **`chart::draw` is dead in the shipped client and its projection loses a
  third of the chart** (R8). Two separable pieces: the deadness (is the
  chart meant to come back, or should it go?) and the projection defect
  (rings 1 and 2 collide in the cardinal directions; the collision winner
  is chosen by facet id). Neither costs a player anything today.
- **Night has no gradation** (R7): `MAX_AIRMASS = 38.0` makes every
  elevation below ~1.5 degrees identical, and nothing moonlit or starlit
  contributes to any illuminant.
- **The habitat descriptor's light word is clock-blind** (R7):
