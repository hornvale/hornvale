# The Cruck — decision ledger

Campaign: **The Cruck** — The Staple's R2, "a building's shape has a
reason." Branch: `campaign/the-cruck`. Decision block: **0786–0795**
(reserved on lefford 2026-09-04; main ceiling was 0750 at reservation).
Worktree: `.claude/worktrees/the-cruck`, taken from main at `3007f164c` (The
Weft's merge product), the same day.

A cruck is the pair of curved timbers that gives a timber building its
section: the frame is the reason the roof has the shape it has. The name is
the claim — a structure's chamber graph is derived from what stands there,
never drawn.

## Entries

#1 [G1] — **How should a structure's chamber graph acquire a reason?** ·
**Decision: run the pattern-language discipline one band coarser. A small
ordered structure-band inventory whose kinds are chamber ROLES and whose
attachment is `Beside(role)` is walked once against the brief; the result is
a rooted tree at the threshold, and the seed fills only what the grammar
leaves free (which facets, and the rectilinear cut positions). Wild sites
(cave, exotic) keep today's drawn chain under the unchanged `room/chambers/v1`
label, because there the derivation runs the other way — the rock made the
form and a people reads it (The Plat's "use follows form").** · Why: Rose
Window §1a.6 ("a people's houses look alike") and §1b.5 ("two bands, one
composer, one validator") already fix the architecture; The Blocking proved
the shape one band finer and The Housemark transposed culture onto it
without a per-people table. The Staple's measurement (its §1) fixes the
inputs: `function`, `notability` and `tech` are constant over 4,002
occupations, so a grammar indexed on them alone would emit one shape; the
axes that vary among living dwellings are `cold`, the housemark's two axes,
and the site kind. Space syntax's justified permeability graph (Hillier and
Hanson) is the domain that already names what a tree topology reads:
DEPTH from the entry is control, BREADTH off the entry is commonality — so
authority and threshold posture have a genuine spatial consequence, not a
decorative one. · Alternatives discarded: (a) keep the path graph and
derive only chamber count and role order — the "reason" then lives in a
length, and a hall-house and a longhouse are both a corridor; (b) a
constraint solver admitting cycles (courtyard rings) — the BSP allocator
cannot realize a cycle and the grower's separation rule was built for a
chain, so cycles are a new embedder, not a grammar row; parked for R3 or
later; (c) read `flesh::structures_of` (Hut/Granary/Longhouse) as the
reason — that is a SETTLEMENT-scale composition answering "which buildings
exist", R3's subject, and six of its eight variants are unreachable for the
same reason the Staple measured. · ideonomy passes / overturns: **2 passes,
1 partial overturn, several material additions.** Pass 1
(tree-finding, cross-domain re-instantiation; notation; cardinality,
modularity, longevity) overturned two parts of the input recommendation:
the "household-size draw" is dropped (rule 4 of the Staple metaplan —
derived or absent — and §1a.7's residual-DOF rule leave a fully-derived
topology nothing to draw), and `Within` is inexpressible at the structure
band (a chamber cannot contain a chamber; nesting IS `Beside(hearthroom)`).
It added: chemistry's ISOMER as the thing R2 makes exist (same chambers,
different links) and the count of shapes it can reach (rooted labelled trees
on ≤4 nodes: at most 16, and the grammar reaches four — the count is
incidental, the reason is the deliverable, spec §4.3 of The Blocking); the
three seams the design must name (grammar → tree, tree → facets, tree →
lattice; today the first two are fused and the third assumes a chain); a
stream-label split by method rather than a bump (#3); and the
`hornvale_history::flesh::Structure` / `hornvale_vessel::structure::Structure`
name collision as a follow-up. Pass 2 (abstraction-lift; procedure;
animacy, direction) added the spec's spine — form follows use for built
sites, use follows form for wild ones, which is why the cave keeps its chain
by argument and not by omission — and dropped an explicit per-role valency
in favour of the existing `MAX_CHAMBERS` bound plus an ordered inventory
(#6). No overturn in pass 2. · Capture actions: follow-ups below; registry
rows at close (`SOC-staple-ladder` R2 slice, `CLIENT-district-patterns`,
a new row for the wild-site role reading).

#2 [Q] — **Reuse `interior::compose` or write a sibling?** · **Decision: a
sibling, typed to `Structure`.** `compose` produces an `Interior` — anchors
with RCC8 relations — and a chamber is not an anchor; reusing it would be a
type pun and would inherit one behaviour the structure band must NOT have:
`compose` attaches a pattern whose target kind is absent to the hub
(`first_of.get(&k).copied().or(hub)`), silently. At the structure band an
absent parent must REFUSE admission (the rule is a `requires`, not a
fallback), or a room would hang off the door for a reason nobody stated.
The discipline is shared — one ordered inventory, admission by the place,
attach to the first-of-kind, connected by construction — and the code is
twenty-five lines. · Why: Rose Window §1b.5 says "one composer" and means
the DISCIPLINE (The Blocking §4.1: "same composer, a different declared
vocabulary"); decision 0025 (one concept name, one owner) argues against
making `Interior` mean two things. · Alternatives discarded: generalise
`compose` over a trait — a premature abstraction for two call sites with
different fallback semantics. · ideonomy passes / overturns: 1 pass
(the notation organon in #1's pass 1 surfaced the fallback as a required
slot), no overturn. · Capture: spec §3.

#3 [Q] — **What happens to `room/chambers/v1`?** · **Decision: it is not
bumped. Wild sites keep drawing under it byte-for-byte (count, then one
draw per chamber). Built sites draw their facets under a NEW label,
`room/chambers/built/v1` (one draw per chamber, no count draw). The
manifest gains one row; no existing row changes.** · Why: decision 0073
declares labels split by blast radius, and The Blocking's §3.2 did exactly
this for the two layout methods ("each method gets its own stream label";
"a change to something the methods share bumps both"). Here the shared
thing — `child_path`'s two-bits-per-digit address extension — is untouched,
and the wild derivation is unchanged, so bumping the wild path would move
every cave for no reason. Verified before deciding: no committed fact
carries a chamber-depth address — every `pack()` in `session.rs` packs a
walk-band room (`settlement_room`, `position()`), and `chamber_id` feeds
only the plan and the `[chamber N]` header — so a changed built derivation
orphans nothing (The Blocking §5.1's closing window is still open on
2026-09-04). · Alternatives discarded: (a) bump to `v2` for both paths —
moves caves gratuitously; (b) keep `v1` and change the built path's
consumption under it — a label whose draw sequence changed without a
version is the exact thing 0073 forbids. · ideonomy passes / overturns: 1
pass (#1 pass 1's modularity prompt: where do the seams want to be), no
overturn. · Capture: spec §6; G3 flag 1.

#4 [Q] — **Does the seed still draw the chamber COUNT for a built site?** ·
**Decision: no. Count is the number of rules the brief admits, capped by
`MAX_CHAMBERS`.** · Why: Staple metaplan standing rule 4 ("a function, a
notability, or a tier is derived or it is absent") and Rose Window §1a.7
("the seed fills exactly the residual degrees of freedom"). A count drawn
uniformly from 1..4 was never a residual of anything; it was the absence of
a reason. One consequence is stated rather than hidden: the single-chamber
dwelling disappears from built sites, because a hearthroom is always
admitted — and that removes an absurdity, since today a one-chamber cold
dwelling has no fire at all (the fire is confined to the hearthroom role).
· Alternatives discarded: a seed-drawn "household size" that admits the
store — plausible, and exactly the kind of parameter rule 4 forbids until
something derives it; if a later campaign puts household size on the
brief, the store's admission row is where it plugs in. · ideonomy passes /
overturns: 1 pass (#1 pass 1's cardinality prompt), overturned the input
recommendation. · Capture: spec §3.2.

#5 [Q] — **Is `Within` an attachment at the structure band?** · **Decision:

· Why: the notation forced the slot and it had no meaning. · Alternatives
discarded: none worth the line. · ideonomy passes / overturns: 1 pass

#6 [Q] — **How is the tree's degree bounded?** · **Decision: by the ordered

#7 [Q] — **What does "further in" mean at a fork?** · **Decision: with one
child it is unchanged; with several it is a refusal that names the ways,

loomroom`) or, as today, a unique prose noun. The existing "noun match only
where exactly one aperture" restriction relaxes to "unique among this
chamber's apertures".** · Why: `Session::further_in` picks the lowest-numbered
higher neighbour, which is unambiguous on a chain and a silent guess at a
fork; the session's own footer text already says a fixed "two ways" would be
"a lie told to a real player" under a richer topology. Siblings never share
a role (one rule per role), so role nouns are unique among apertures by
construction. · Alternatives discarded: ordinal ways ("first way", "second
way") — an index is not a reason and the player cannot see the index. ·
ideonomy passes / overturns: 1 pass (direction prompt, pass 2), no overturn.
· Capture: spec §5.3.

## Follow-ups

- **Two `Structure`s.** `hornvale_history::flesh::Structure`
  (Hut/Granary/Longhouse/…: which buildings a settlement has) and
  `hornvale_vessel::structure::Structure` (the chamber graph of the one
  building a facet holds) share a name and answer different questions
  (decision 0025). Not renamed here — the history one is consumed by the
  almanac and two ID-invariance suites — but R3, which will need both in
  one sentence, should rename one.
- **A wild structure's roles should be a reading, not an index** (#8): The
  Plat's entry/heart/sanctum over a chain. Registry row at close.
- **A ruin does not remember its builder's grammar** (CLIENT-housemark-
  provenance, unchanged): a ruined dwelling's shape is derived from its
  brief as it stands. The longevity substitution in pass 1 says the shape
  should outlive the occupant; nothing on the brief carries the builder,
  so nothing here claims it.
- **A mark that changes a shape** (a barred doorway, rubble in an aperture)
  is the first chamber-depth commit, and decision 0084's clause and The
  Blocking §5.1 already say what that costs. Out of scope; noted so the
  label split in #3 is read with that window in mind.
- **`plan_of` receives `chambers.len()` as `of`** and the client renders
  "chamber i of n"; with a tree, "of n" is still true and "i" is still an
  index. Unchanged, but the number no longer implies a position on a line.
