# The Warp — decision ledger

Campaign: `campaign/the-warp` · Spec:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-the-warp-design.md` · Decision block:
**0776–0785** (reserved under `the-warp`, the name the branch submits from).

The successor The Weft named: *"start from what a walker should be able to
infer, and let frequency fall out of it."* The Weft's own chronicle opens by
defining the **warp** as the macro fields already running continuously
through the world and the **weft** as the fine detail crossing them. The
Weft wove the detail. This campaign makes the warp visible through it — a
walker can only read a sign that is rendered — and measures legibility from
the walker's side rather than the world's.

---

### #1 [G1] — the three candidates are components, not alternatives

**Question.** How does the inverted campaign start? Three candidates were on
the table: (A) render the hidden causes as walker-visible signs and
re-measure legibility over rendered sign tokens; (B) leave the prose alone
and build a learner-shaped instrument (a walker who tabulates sign → feature
over walks) to settle `SURF-legibility-ranking-may-be-estimator-resolution`;
(C) invert the recipes so each kind is defined by the sign conjunction a
walker should infer it from, with abundance falling out.

**Decision.** A and B together, as two halves of one instrument chain; C is
**not** a code change at this entry (overturned at #3, below, by
measurement) — "frequency falls out" is an accounting identity the
instrument reports, `P(Y) = P(class)·rate + P(¬class)·floor`.

**Why (precedent cited).** Decision 0016 (freeze before measurement), 0687
(a derived surface reads continuous causes, never categorical labels — so a
recipe must not read a rendered word), `KNOW-uncertainty-is-epistemic` (the
criterion: a place is *found* iff a knowledgeable observer could have
predicted it from visible signs and a naive one could not — which names
two observers and a channel, none of which the Weft's H3 modelled), and
The Rill (a rendered axis grounded within the headroom an address draw
leaves, draw order preserved as a save-format contract).

**Alternatives discarded.** C-as-recipe-inversion: reading a sign word in a
recipe violates 0687 and would make a feature a function of prose. B alone:
a learner over prose that renders no cause can only learn the biome, which
the probe (#2) then confirmed. A alone: a channel reading over a
high-cardinality tuple is finite-sample bias unless paired with a null.

**ideonomy passes / overturns: 2 / 1.** Pass 1 (combination × substitution,
dictionary; decomposability, discovery-vs-invention, polarity):
*decomposability* split legibility into five parts — the cause exists at
facet scale; the feature tracks it (the Weft's H3, a **ceiling**); the cause
is **rendered** (a channel); the rendering is **learnable** by a finite
observer; and the rendered word is **honest** (a noise-drawn word with
causal semantics is a *false sign*, the polarity flip). A, B and C each
addressed one part — the overturn. *Discovery-vs-invention* put the signs
on the discovered side: the causes are already there; the campaign stops
hiding them, and 0687 stays intact because the sign is a level set of the
same continuous cause the recipe reads. *Substitution* on "who
discretizes" (the estimator's four bins → the language's own thresholds →
the observer's own cut points) surfaced that a rich sign tuple inflates MI,
so **every reading is paired with a permutation-style null** — adopted.
The dictionary organon fixed the vocabulary the spec uses (§2 there).
Pass 2 (organon-construction, spectrum; predictability, visibility): the
legibility spectrum has an upper failure as well as a lower one — *told*
(the sign restates the feature, wallpaper) sits above *found*, so the
preregistration freezes a **band**, not a floor. *Visibility* found the
criterion's own fork: a sign visible to all, with the *reading* held only
by the knowledgeable — the naive observer sees the same words and has no
table; a species-gated reading is captured, not built. No overturn on
pass 2; converged.

*Capture:* this entry; spec §1–§3; follow-ups list below (species-gated
reading, co-occurrence legibility).

### #2 [Q] — measure the premise before writing it (2026-09-05)

**Question.** Is "the walker's signs today are either uniform over ~120 km
or address noise" a belief or a number?

**Decision.** A number. `windows/lab/tests/suite/warp_probe.rs` (committed,
`#[ignore = "probe: …"]`, 1.2 s) tabulates every rendered sign at seed 42
against each kind's occurrence over the Weft's own land-eligible
population, each reading beside a lagged null, and reproduces the Weft's
H3 readings exactly as its positive control.

**What it found.** Net of null, in bits: thicket is already legible at its
ceiling from the biome word alone (0.039 of 0.039); spring reads 0.0025 of
a 0.0078 ceiling from what is rendered and 0.0061 with the (unrendered)
rock word added; the three address-noise axes read at their nulls; the
whole sentence as one tuple reads 0.15–0.37 bits and so does its null. And
the decisive table: **333 of 403 springs stand on a facet whose cause bin
is the lowest** — the sign case is 92% noise-born by recipe, because its
cause class is 4% of land and the floor `abundance × (1 − contextuality) ×
noise` runs over the other 96%. The falsified `spring > thicket` ordering
reproduces on the 19-class biome word, so it was never the four-bin
estimator.

**Why.** `measure-before-calling-it-a-campaign`, The Staple (a probe's
numbers are its load-bearing structure — commit it), and campaign-autopilot's
rule that a claim about generated output needs a command-and-output pair
before it enters a spec.

*ideonomy passes / overturns:* none run for this entry — it is a
measurement, not a ruling; the rulings it changed are #3 and #5.

*Capture:* the probe file's module doc carries the full readout; spec §1.

### #3 [Q] — the ceiling is a recipe property, so the recipe moves (overturns #1's "recipes unchanged")

**Question.** `SURF-legibility-ranking-may-be-estimator-resolution` says
"do not retune either recipe to answer it". The Weft's retrospective says a
recipe change "needs a new design/readout". Does this campaign change the
recipes?

**Decision.** Yes, by **re-parameterisation, not retuning**: a kind's
prevalence becomes `rate_k · cause + floor_k · noise` (cause still the
continuous `macro_state`, 0687 intact), where `floor_k` is authored by
intent per kind — near zero for the sign kinds (spring, overhang), kept for
the texture kind (thicket — texture exists to break monotony, not to be
inferred), and the whole of it for the erratic (the control). Abundance
stops being a dial; frequency is a *reported consequence*. The registry
row's prohibition was against tuning to rescue a number; this is a new
design with its own frozen readout, which is the route the Weft's own
retrospective names.

**Why (precedent cited).** #2's table: the found fraction — the share of a
kind's occurrences standing on a facet whose cause reads ≥ 0.5 — is 0.077
for spring and 0.057 for overhang, and no rendering can raise it, because
rendering changes what the walker sees, not where the feature is.
`UNI-appraisal` supplies the form: this is signal-detection's base-rate
effect, a small false-alarm rate over a large population outnumbering a
high hit rate over a small one, and the found fraction is the positive
predictive value.

**Alternatives discarded.** Leave the recipes and accept a 92%-extruded
sign case (then the successor campaign inherits the same ceiling). Retune
`SPRING_CONTEXTUALITY` toward 1.0 inside the Weft's parameterisation
(possible, but `abundance × contextuality` keeps two dials for one
quantity, and the floor stays coupled to abundance; the re-parameterisation
names the two quantities the readout actually measures).

**ideonomy passes / overturns: 1 / 1.** Abstraction-lift (lattice organon;
symmetry, homogeneity): stripped, the recipe is *a thinned point process
whose intensity is a mixture of a signal term and a signal-free term*.
Re-instantiated in detection theory it is hit rate plus false-alarm rate,
and PPV depends on the cause's base rate — the mechanism #2 measured.
*Symmetry:* the mixture weights treat signal and noise symmetrically while
their populations are 4% and 96% of land, so `contextuality = 0.85` reads
"85% caused" and yields 8% caused occurrences. *Homogeneity:* one floor
over all land is the defect; the inverted recipe makes the floor zero
outside the cause class. The lattice orders the kinds by (floor, cause
prevalence) and predicts the found-fraction ordering the spec freezes.

*Capture:* this entry; spec §6; decision 0777 (expected).

### #4 [Q] — what a sign is, and what is deliberately not one

**Question.** Which rendered things count as signs for the instrument, and
does the `examine` datum (which prints "12.3 °C the year round, moisture
0.42") count?

**Decision.** A sign is a **rendered, world-determined, at-facet, discrete
token**: a word the walk-band sentence emits whose value is a function of
the world at that facet. The instrument reads exactly the tuple the sentence
renders — biome word, rock word (new), steepness word (new), wetness word —
and nothing else. The `examine` datum's continuous numbers are excluded: a
walker "sees" temperature only through the biome word, and that datum is a
diagnostic leak of two raw fields (captured as a registry row, not fixed
here). Rumors are excluded as before (the Weft deferred them with their
producer; `SURF-rumors-need-a-producer-before-the-region-invariant`).

**Why.** Decision 0076 (the situated pole is egocentric and
knowledge-limited), 0349 (an offer passes through the observer's knowledge
before it is rendered) — the walker's channel is the prose. Measuring with
the published predicate (memory): the tokens are the renderer's own
thresholds, so the estimator has no bin parameter to argue about.

**ideonomy passes / overturns: 1 / 0.** Negation (state-machine organon;
connectivity, complexity). Negating each definitional property named a
sibling: *not rendered* → the hidden cause (the Weft's variable, kept as the
ceiling); *not world-determined* → the **false sign** (the noun, relief,
aspect and openness — address noise wearing causal semantics; measured as a
control, not deleted, see #6); *not at the facet* → the **remote sign** (a
neighbouring facet's word, the rill band, a rumor — a follow-up row, since
the learner instrument could read lag-1 signs cheaply later); *not discrete*
→ the continuous datum (excluded, above). *Connectivity:* biome and wetness
both read moisture, so they count as one channel (`KNOW-channel-
independence`) — the reason the joint tuple, not the sum of singles, is the
statistic. *Complexity:* the full sentence has 5,840 classes over 11,218
facets — the estimator's enemy, hence the null. The state machine (unseen →
seen → tabulated → predictive; never stale, the world is static) is what the
learner instrument walks.

*Capture:* this entry; spec §2, §5.1; follow-up rows (remote signs, the
examine leak).

### #5 [Q] — who the observers are, and what they report

**Question.** "A knowledgeable observer could have predicted it and a naive
one could not" — what are the two observers, mechanically?

**Decision.** Three observers, one instrument: the **naive** observer
predicts the base rate; the **oracle** observer predicts `P(Y | signs)`
from the whole land-eligible population (the channel reading, MI net of
null); the **learner** fits the same table on a training half and predicts
on a held-out half (world-split), reported as log-loss gain over the naive
observer; and, descriptively, the same learner on the Weft's walk pool at
increasing step counts (a learning curve, not gated). Readouts per kind:
ceiling MI, channel MI net of null, efficiency = channel/ceiling, found
fraction (PPV), best-class lift at a support floor, learner gain. Across
seeds as a distribution (min and median over the readout seeds), never a
point.

**Why.** The Surmise (belief is a lazily populated cache over perceived
facts — an observer is a table built from experience); `KNOW-deduction-gain`
(gain over the joint, not the marginals); `freeze-a-distribution-not-a-
point` and `preregister-on-a-count-not-a-ratio` (memories).

**ideonomy passes / overturns: 1 / 0.** Cross-domain re-instantiation (tree
organon; distribution, naturalness). The abstract form — a predictor fitted
on a sample, scored against a majority baseline — re-instantiated in
diagnostic medicine gives sensitivity/specificity/PPV and the base-rate
fallacy (adopted: PPV *is* the found fraction); in ecology, indicator
species; in ML, the held-out split and the majority-class baseline
(adopted). The tree's siblings: a **misled** observer who trusts false signs
— not modelled, because it needs authored semantics; the false-sign control
(#6) is the honest version. *Naturalness:* the oracle's table is discovered,
the learner's is constructed, and their gap as a function of experience is
the learning curve.

*Capture:* this entry; spec §5.

### #6 [Q] — the address-noise axes: add words, never re-ground words

**Question.** Relief, aspect and openness are address noise, and so is the
descriptor noun's variety draw. Ground them, delete them, or leave them?

**Decision.** Leave them, measure them. This campaign **adds** two grounded
words (rock, steepness) and re-grounds nothing. The noise axes and the noun
are read by the instrument as the **false-sign baseline** — they must sit
at their nulls, which is a control that the instrument is honest, and a
learner assigns them no weight, which is what a walker does with a word that
never pays.

**Why.** The Rill: the draw order is a save-format contract (every axis is
spent); grounding wetness within its headroom moved the emitted axis by
three orders of magnitude less than the draw, and no tuning rescued the
prediction. The micro-field's own doc states its purpose — room-to-room
variety under a homogeneous biome — so those words are texture by design.
Relief has a real cause available since The Hachure (sub-vertex height) but
no kind reads relief, so grounding it would make a sign of nothing this
campaign measures. Openness could be grounded on productivity, but thicket
already reads at its ceiling from the biome word, so it would buy nothing
measurable.

**ideonomy passes / overturns: 1 / 0.** Substitution (cycle organon;
purpose, reversibility). Substituting the axes' *source* (noise → grounded →
suppressed) and asking their *purpose* separated texture from sign; the
cycle — sign → inference → walk → confirmation → trust — closes for a false
sign in *learned distrust*, which the learner measures for free.
*Reversibility:* adding a word is reversible and moves only rendering;
re-grounding an axis moves every fixture and is what The Rill already spent
a campaign on.

*Capture:* this entry; spec §4.3; registry row for relief-on-Hachure.

### #7 [Q] — cost, and one implementation of every sign

**Question.** New metrics run on every world of every `"all"` study forever
(lab CLAUDE.md). What does the instrument cost, and where do the sign
functions live?

**Decision.** The sign functions are **one implementation** each, read by
the prose and by the lab — the prose's rock word and the lab's rock token
call the same function on the same dominant-corner read, so the two cannot
drift (The Rill's own warning about a second copy of a partition). The lab
extends the Weft's existing grid pool with sign tokens rather than adding a
sweep. Decision rule: measure the added per-world cost in release on the
Mac before registering; ≤ 0.25 CPU-s/world added (a third of the Weft pool's
measured 0.75) registers as-is; more subsamples the pool for the sign
columns, and the spec records which.

**Why.** Lab CLAUDE.md §"Registering a metric is not a local act" (cost
lives in sweeping; a second sweep is the failure); the Weft's Task 9 fix
round (share one context, never build it twice). The probe's whole sweep,
including 40,962 prose renders it will not need, was 1.2 s unoptimized.

**ideonomy passes / overturns: 1 / 0.** Cross-domain re-instantiation
(atlas organon; age, hierarchicalness). An astronomical survey does not add
a filter to the all-sky cadence for one target — but here there is no
opt-out (`"all"`), so the analogue is the *pool*: a shared exposure read by
many leaf metrics. *Hierarchicalness:* leaves on an existing pool are the
cheap shape; a new pool is the expensive one. *Age:* the Weft's 22 metrics
are one day old and already restaged every fixture; this campaign's land
in the same census refresh, so the fixture cost is paid once.

*Capture:* this entry; spec §5.4.

### #10 [G5, Task 5] — three instrument defects the implementer found, ruled before any readout seed exists

**Question.** Task 5 returned DONE_WITH_CONCERNS: (a) the added census cost
is 0.331 CPU-s/world against the spec's 0.25 rule, all of it the wetness
sign's rill query; (b) H4's ±0.001 bars sit below the permutation null's own
standard deviation (0.002 bits at 469 classes); (c) the learner's Laplace
smoothing reads NEGATIVE gains for spring (−0.049) and the oracle's in-sample
gain is negative too, so H3's "≥ 0.5 × oracle" has no meaning.

**Decision.** (a) Accept 0.331 as-is; do not subsample — halving `n` doubles
the null's bias and widens its spread from 0.0020 to 0.0035, blunting the
controls to save ~23 s of census wall. Spec §5.4 amended with the measurement
and the falsified premise ("three map reads"). (b) H4's bars become four null
standard deviations, fixed from seed 42: erratic channel net ≤ 0.008
(one-sided), false-sign net within ±0.002. (c) The learner shrinks toward the
BASE RATE with an equivalent-sample-size prior `α = 10`, `(k + α·p̂)/(n + α)`,
so an uninformative class scores 0 and never below; the "≥ 0.5 × oracle"
clause is withdrawn from H3's gate and the ratio learner/(channel net) is
reported instead; `warp-oracle-gain-*` stays registered. (d, found by the
correction round) H3's erratic learner bar becomes one-sided, ≤ 0.001: a
held-out table over ~469 classes of noise must LOSE to the base rate, so
"within ±0.001" was unreachable by construction; the control is that the
erratic never gains.

**Why (precedent cited).** Decision 0016's ordering: an instrument
correction made before the readout seeds are built, with the confounding
figures stated inline, is the ordinary correction of an instrument — the
Weft amended H3's population the same way before Task 9 ran. Memory
`a-negative-control-must-assert-over-something-constructed`: a control that
fails by noise a third of the time is not a control. The positive control
that licenses (b): the χ² prediction of the null's mean, 0.030091 bits,
equals `warp-channel-null-erratic`'s reading to five places.

**Alternatives discarded.** Subsampling per the letter of §5.4 (degrades the
instrument the rule exists to protect). Dropping the wetness sign (loses the
one grounded micro word; the cost is a per-facet rill query already paid by
`describe`, not new work). Keeping Laplace and gating on it (an artefact of
the prior, not a property of the world).

**ideonomy passes / overturns:** none — three record-and-instrument
corrections against a converged design; no design choice reopened.

*Capture:* spec §5.2, §5.4, §7 (H3, H4) amended and dated; the Task 5 fix
round carries (b) and (c) into code; the retro carries the a/b-design lesson
(two studies sharing one pool cannot measure that pool's cost).

### #11 [G5, Task 6] — a bar met by making a kind rarer is withdrawn; reliability set in the middle of the passing range

**Question.** Calibration round 3 satisfied H2's "spring net ≥ overhang net"
by cutting `OVERHANG_RATE` to 0.16 — twelve times rarer than the Weft's
overhang — while spring's channel reading did not move. Is the clause sound?

**Decision.** No; withdrawn before any readout seed is built (spec §7 H2
amended, dated). MI in bits scales with H(Y), so a between-kind ordering
rewards rarity; each kind is gated on its own legibility instead, and the
ordering is reported raw and normalised. Overhang re-calibrated on its own
bands: 0.50 and 0.75 hold every band, 0.90 trips H5 (max class rate 0.761);
`OVERHANG_RATE = 0.50`, the middle of the range — H5 headroom 1.62× against
1.17× at 0.75, and a sign that pays one time in two where its cause saturates
is found-not-told, where 0.75 sits against the wallpaper guard by
construction. Spring stays as frozen (rate 0.95, step (0.35, 0.55)): its
rarity — existence density 0.0359 → 0.0134, encounter rate 0 on the 78-walk
pool because the pool's largest spring cause is ~0.244 under a 0.35 edge — is
the magnitude of the fidelity trade G3 flag 1 named, and goes to G6 as such.

**Why (precedent cited).** Decision 0016 (an instrument correction before
the readout, with the confounding figures inline — three rungs of one
constant with spring identical in all three is the figure); memory
`preregistration-contains-metric-chasing` and `a-floor-without-a-ceiling`;
`code-satisfies-a-criterion-a-player-may-not` (the walk-band zero is
reported, not tuned away).

**Alternatives discarded.** Keep the clause and accept overhang at 0.16 (a
kind removed to pass a bar about another). Normalise the clause by H(Y)
(still gameable by rarity in the other direction). Take the highest passing
rung, 0.75 (thin H5 headroom; near "told").

**ideonomy passes / overturns:** none — a correction of a defective bar, not
a design choice reopened.

*Capture:* spec §7 (H1 note, H2 amendment); the Weft's `weft_density.rs`
`spring > overhang` cause-MI witness was re-pinned twice inside one day as

spring 0.0865 throughout) and holds at 0.50; the retro carries "highest
passing rung" as the wrong calibration criterion.
