# The Pawl — decision ledger

Campaign: `campaign/the-pawl` · Spec:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-01-the-pawl-design.md` · Plan: (not yet
written) · Decision block: 0536–0545 (reserved on lefford 2026-09-01; main
ceiling was 0516 at reservation).

A pawl is the catch that lets a ratchet hold what it has gained and only
ever advance. The name is the mechanism: a fold advances and is never
invalidated (decision 0236), and the campaign's job is to make the
liveness stack stand on folds instead of re-walking the trail.

Committed per The Cartulary (decision 0486): every ruling is written here
as it happens, on this branch, in this worktree.

## #1 [G1] — which carve of the remaining log-bounding work is this campaign?

**Question.** The Tailrace (7a) shipped `kernel/src/fold.rs` with zero
tenants and carved its own stages 3–5 as "need their own plan". Decision
0238 puts 7b (the typed intention, an epoch) and 7c (fact lifetime) after
it in a forced order. Nathan's row `UNI-ecs-is-the-adaptive-cache`
(2026-08-31) says the ECS layer is a data-oriented cache over DERIVED
data, and tick-by-tick position and hysteresis state belong in it. Which
of these is The Pawl?

**Decision.** The Pawl is **Tailrace stages 3–5, plus the thing that makes
them one campaign rather than six edits: a session-owned, per-entity
resident store of `Folded` states — the first concrete instance of the
layer Nathan's row names.** Byte-identical. 7b is OUT and is the
recommended next campaign; 7c stays behind 7b per 0238. Hysteresis on
drive arbitration (`PSY-drive-arbitration-limit-cycle`) is OUT as a
behaviour change, but the store is where its state will live and the
spec says so.

**Why (precedent cited).**

- Tailrace §6 stage table: stages 3–5 are enterable, gate met, blocker
  gone (The Escapement landed 2026-08-25). Verified today: `grep -c
  'Folded<\|LedgerFold' windows/vessel/src/liveness.rs` → 0.
- Tailrace §5 already FROZE the readout (H2: `drive_at` median elasticity
  < 0.20 on runs with r² ≥ 0.5; `C` identifiable and positive; whole-tick
  history share < 20% against 70–80%). Inheriting it verbatim is the
  preregistration discipline (decision 0016); re-freezing would be
  metric-chasing.
- The Tackle / The Deed precedent: a "nothing moved" campaign and an
  "artifacts moved" campaign destroy each other's legibility when
  bundled. 7b moves rendered prose (the per-step `provenance` is content —
  0238's own argument) and costs an epoch; stages 3–5 move nothing. Split.
- Penstock §6.6, Nathan 2026-08-23: "keep the system general so we're
  sure to be able to use it for whatever comes up." A store with six
  tenants is the general object; six private caches are not.

**Alternatives discarded.**

- *Stages 3–5 alone, no store.* Six functions each grow a private
  `Folded` and a private place to keep it. Rejected: that is six
  half-designs of the same store, and the second campaign to need one
  (hysteresis, 7b's intention anchor) would build a seventh.
- *Stages 3–5 plus 7b.* Rejected for the Tackle/Deed reason above, and
  because 7b's design (what `Intention` is, how `why?` composes) has its
  own G3-sized questions that would crowd this one's.
- *Jump to 7c.* Forbidden by 0238's forced order while anything folds raw
  history — which is exactly the state this campaign ends.
- *ECS metaplan campaign 7 (spatial partition).* Trigger not met: ledger
  queries profiled at 0.04% of a tick (Penstock §6.5).

**Ideonomy passes / overturns.** One `ideonomy-plain` pass (operators:
substitution, tree-finding; organon: graph; prompts: discovery-vs-
invention, modularity, age). No overturn of the carve; two enrichments
adopted into the design:

1. **The seam is at READ, not at COMMIT.** Substituting "who advances the
   fold" over {commit hook, session after commit, lazily on read}: the
   Tailrace's own §11 records that `Ledger::commit` gained no hook by
   design, and `Session::wait` evaluates the walk TWICE (once through
   `step_with_occupancy`, once through `hornvale_kernel::tick` for the
   facts it commits — `agent_scaling.rs`'s module doc). A fold advanced
   per EVALUATION would double-absorb; a fold advanced per COMMIT needs
   the hook that was refused. A fold advanced lazily on read —
   `advance_to(ledger)` before every read — is idempotent in position, so
   both evaluations and every re-read cost O(new facts) and nothing else,
   and the in-tick `out` overlay `decide_step` already folds on top of
   `frozen` stays exactly as it is.
2. **The level slide.** Walking the tree up from "six folds" reaches "the
   per-entity resident state store" one level up, and that is the object
   that outlives the campaign. The store is keyed by `EntityId`, lives in
   the session (never the save — 0069, Tailrace §7), and a tenant is a
   `LedgerFold` impl plus a key. Hysteresis state and 7b's intention
   anchor are future tenants, not future stores.

The graph's hidden hub was the **commit site** (`out.push(agent_at_fact)`
at the walk's `MoveTo` arm): every fold advances past it, 7b changes what
is pushed there, 7c abstains there. Nothing in this campaign touches it,
and the spec says so as a determinism contract.

**Capture actions.**

- `PSY-drive-arbitration-limit-cycle`: the registry row gains a `Where`
  pointer to this spec's store as the home for hysteresis state
  (registry edit at spec commit).
- `UNI-intention-is-structured` (7b): unchanged; this ledger names it as
  the recommended next campaign.
- The trap-5 semantics question (Tailrace §2: today's reset lookup has no
  `<= t` filter) becomes a **decision rule** in the spec, not a
  prediction — see #2.

## #2 [Q] — where does resident state live, and how do `&self` readers reach it?

**Question.** `Session` holds `mesh_memo` and `home_nav_cache` across ticks,
but `Session::snapshot(&self)` cannot reach them and today constructs
throwaway `PrimaryAfraidMemo`, `RoomMeshMemo` and `HomeNavCache` per call
(`session.rs`, the `snapshot` body). A throwaway fold store would be an
O(history) rebuild per creature per turn.

**Decision.** The store is a `Session` field beside the two existing memos,
equally threadable by the benches (`agent_scaling.rs`,
`session_length_scaling.rs` drive `step_with_occupancy` with caller-owned
caches). `&self` readers reach it through interior mutability (`RefCell`),
advancing on read. A throwaway rebuild is refused (spec §2.2, rule 3).

**Why.** Penstock §6.6 ("keep it general"); Tailrace §7 (no serialized
surface; no memory budget because there is no eviction); `clippy.toml` bans
`HashMap`/`HashSet` and wall-clock, not `RefCell`. The per-turn snapshot is
the `CLIENT-one-snapshot` emit and runs for every creature — a rebuild there
would keep the quadratic term on the path H2 does not measure.

**Alternatives discarded.** Serialize the store (0069, Tailrace §7 forbid
it, and Nathan's row says persisting buys startup time, not truth — a later
question, not this campaign's); a free function taking `&mut Session`
(changes every `&self` reader's signature for one field).

**Ideonomy passes / overturns.** Resolved inside pass 1's substitution on
"who advances the fold" (ledger #1); one pass, no overturn.

**Capture.** None beyond the spec.

## #3 [Q] — how is a past-instant read served, and which tenants ever get one?

**Question.** `Folded` only advances. The Tailrace assumed past-`t` reads
were catch-up's replay loop. Reading the code: `hazard_memory_memo` →
`emitter_arousal` → `affect_of(m, day)` → `drive_at(m, day)` at every
visited room's latest-visit day, per emitter, per tick — a past-instant read
of the thirst fold **on the hot path, inside the most expensive fold**
(73–97 ms/call at the final band).

**Decision.** Sustenance keeps a checkpoint list (the state at each reset)
and serves a past read by resume-from-last-checkpoint-≤-t plus advance over
the Trail range `(reset, t]` — decision 0237 made concrete, minted as 0539.
Set-shaped tenants (KnownWater, LatestVisit) get NO bounded past read; the
spec's rule 6 makes their absence from hot paths a witnessed fact, not an
assumption, and names the fallback shape if the witness finds one.

**Why.** 0237 states the rule; the code trace above says which tenant needs
it. The Trail index is needed by three tenants at once, which is what
justifies keeping an O(trail)-sized resident object where the Tailrace ruled
the per-call hub out (spec §2.4, minted as 0538).

**Alternatives discarded.** `rebuild_upto` on the hot path (the O(history)
walk the campaign exists to remove); per-day snapshots of every tenant
(memory O(ticks), and 0237 says the reset is the natural checkpoint).

**Ideonomy passes / overturns.** Pass 2 (`ideonomy-plain`: organon-
construction → dictionary; prompts: autonomy, visibility). The dictionary
of read kinds — current, tick-start-with-overlay, reset-anchored past,
set-shaped past, replay, oracle — showed exactly one entry with no bounded
implementation (set-shaped past) and one whose result is player-visible
through `dread` (reset-anchored past), which is why rule 5 demands the
wrong-checkpoint mutation. No overturn; two rules added.

**Capture.** Spec §3 rules 5 and 6; followups #6.

## #4 [Q] — which folds migrate?

**Decision.** Trail, Sustenance ×2, KnownWater, LatestVisit, Alarm.
`fatigue_at` stays: the Tailrace attribution shows no stable elasticity sign
and a cost three orders of magnitude below the rest. Precedent: the
Tailrace's own stage-4 gate ("if the attribution says belief/hazard do not
carry `k`, migrating them is unmotivated memory") — they do, so they go.
Resolved by the attribution table, inside pass 1's tree walk (children of
"the six folds"); no overturn.

## #5 [G4] — plan self-review against the approved spec

**Question.** Does `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-01-the-pawl.md` implement
every spec section, and did writing it change any spec claim?

**Decision.** Proceed to execution, subagent-driven. One spec change made
and recorded: **§2.3's `Folded<S>: Clone` kernel edit is dropped.** The
overlay (this tick's `out` facts) is a read-side slice argument to
`sustenance_at`, which is what `decide_step` already builds; a tenant's
state is keyed by entity inside ONE fold, so cloning a tenant per creature
per tick would be O(agents) per creature — the O(agents²) shape the campaign
removes. The campaign now makes no kernel edit at all.

**Why.** Spec §2.2's own reasoning (advance once, read many) applied to the
overlay; YAGNI on a kernel `derive`. Precedent for the read-side overlay:
`decide_step`'s existing `frozen` + `out` fold.

**Alternatives discarded.** Per-entity `Folded` instances (a fold per
entity absorbs every ledger fact and ignores most — O(agents × facts) per
advance, and N separate positions to keep honest); cloning a tenant's whole
map per creature (above).

**Ideonomy passes / overturns.** No fresh pass; the ruling is a
consequence of pass 1's "seam at read" finding applied to the overlay, and
the plan's self-review is where it surfaced. Recorded so the omission is
visible rather than silent.

**Capture.** Spec §2.3, §7, §10 amended in the same commit. The plan
executes rules 1–6 as witnesses (T3 s1, T2 s1, T2 s6, T3 s2, T5 s3, T4 s1)
and the byte-identity witness with its positive control is Task 1, BEFORE
any read site moves.
