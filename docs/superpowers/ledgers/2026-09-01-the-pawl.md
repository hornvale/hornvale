# The Pawl — decision ledger

Campaign: `campaign/the-pawl` · Spec:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-01-the-pawl-design.md` · Plan: (not yet
written) · Decision block: 0536–0545 (reserved on lefford 2026-09-01; main
ceiling was 0516 at reservation).

A pawl is the catch that lets a ratchet hold what it has gained and only
ever advance. The name is the mechanism: a fold advances and is never
invalidated (decision 0236), and the campaign's job is to make the
liveness stack stand on folds instead of re-walking the trail.

Committed per The Cartulary (decision 0486): every ruling is written here
as it happens, on this branch, in this worktree.

## #1 [G1] — which carve of the remaining log-bounding work is this campaign?

**Question.** The Tailrace (7a) shipped `kernel/src/fold.rs` with zero
tenants and carved its own stages 3–5 as "need their own plan". Decision
0238 puts 7b (the typed intention, an epoch) and 7c (fact lifetime) after
it in a forced order. Nathan's row `UNI-ecs-is-the-adaptive-cache`
(2026-08-31) says the ECS layer is a data-oriented cache over DERIVED
data, and tick-by-tick position and hysteresis state belong in it. Which
of these is The Pawl?

**Decision.** The Pawl is **Tailrace stages 3–5, plus the thing that makes
them one campaign rather than six edits: a session-owned, per-entity
resident store of `Folded` states — the first concrete instance of the
layer Nathan's row names.** Byte-identical. 7b is OUT and is the
recommended next campaign; 7c stays behind 7b per 0238. Hysteresis on
drive arbitration (`PSY-drive-arbitration-limit-cycle`) is OUT as a
behaviour change, but the store is where its state will live and the
spec says so.

**Why (precedent cited).**

- Tailrace §6 stage table: stages 3–5 are enterable, gate met, blocker
  gone (The Escapement landed 2026-08-25). Verified today: `grep -c
  'Folded<\|LedgerFold' windows/vessel/src/liveness.rs` → 0.
- Tailrace §5 already FROZE the readout (H2: `drive_at` median elasticity
  < 0.20 on runs with r² ≥ 0.5; `C` identifiable and positive; whole-tick
  history share < 20% against 70–80%). Inheriting it verbatim is the
  preregistration discipline (decision 0016); re-freezing would be
  metric-chasing.
- The Tackle / The Deed precedent: a "nothing moved" campaign and an
  "artifacts moved" campaign destroy each other's legibility when
  bundled. 7b moves rendered prose (the per-step `provenance` is content —
  0238's own argument) and costs an epoch; stages 3–5 move nothing. Split.
- Penstock §6.6, Nathan 2026-08-23: "keep the system general so we're
  sure to be able to use it for whatever comes up." A store with six
  tenants is the general object; six private caches are not.

**Alternatives discarded.**

- *Stages 3–5 alone, no store.* Six functions each grow a private
  `Folded` and a private place to keep it. Rejected: that is six
  half-designs of the same store, and the second campaign to need one
  (hysteresis, 7b's intention anchor) would build a seventh.
- *Stages 3–5 plus 7b.* Rejected for the Tackle/Deed reason above, and
  because 7b's design (what `Intention` is, how `why?` composes) has its
  own G3-sized questions that would crowd this one's.
- *Jump to 7c.* Forbidden by 0238's forced order while anything folds raw
  history — which is exactly the state this campaign ends.
- *ECS metaplan campaign 7 (spatial partition).* Trigger not met: ledger
  queries profiled at 0.04% of a tick (Penstock §6.5).

**Ideonomy passes / overturns.** One `ideonomy-plain` pass (operators:
substitution, tree-finding; organon: graph; prompts: discovery-vs-
invention, modularity, age). No overturn of the carve; two enrichments
adopted into the design:

1. **The seam is at READ, not at COMMIT.** Substituting "who advances the
   fold" over {commit hook, session after commit, lazily on read}: the
   Tailrace's own §11 records that `Ledger::commit` gained no hook by
   design, and `Session::wait` evaluates the walk TWICE (once through
   `step_with_occupancy`, once through `hornvale_kernel::tick` for the
   facts it commits — `agent_scaling.rs`'s module doc). A fold advanced
   per EVALUATION would double-absorb; a fold advanced per COMMIT needs
   the hook that was refused. A fold advanced lazily on read —
   `advance_to(ledger)` before every read — is idempotent in position, so
   both evaluations and every re-read cost O(new facts) and nothing else,
   and the in-tick `out` overlay `decide_step` already folds on top of
   `frozen` stays exactly as it is.
2. **The level slide.** Walking the tree up from "six folds" reaches "the
   per-entity resident state store" one level up, and that is the object
   that outlives the campaign. The store is keyed by `EntityId`, lives in
   the session (never the save — 0069, Tailrace §7), and a tenant is a
   `LedgerFold` impl plus a key. Hysteresis state and 7b's intention
   anchor are future tenants, not future stores.

The graph's hidden hub was the **commit site** (`out.push(agent_at_fact)`
at the walk's `MoveTo` arm): every fold advances past it, 7b changes what
is pushed there, 7c abstains there. Nothing in this campaign touches it,
and the spec says so as a determinism contract.
