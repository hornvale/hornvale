# The Weft — decision ledger

The campaign that builds the **derived surface tier**: a per-facet feature
surface computed from noise interacting with macro state, closing the density
gap The Prospect measured. Decision block **0686-0695**.

The name: the **warp** is the macro fields already running continuously through
the world (`blend_at` over per-vertex fields); the **weft** is the fine detail
that crosses them at facet resolution. The weaving metaphor is not decoration —
it is where the campaign's correlation-length parameter came from (entry #2).

---

### #1 [Q, Nathan] — what "something interesting" means at 1.126 km

**Question.** The aim "every square mile, something interesting" is satisfiable
very differently depending on whether the dense thing is a place you *enter* or
something you merely *notice*. No precedent: this is world-design intent, not
derivable from the decision log, the registry or the code. Candidate answers
diverge materially (derived interiors vs. derived prose), so it was surfaced
rather than auto-resolved.

**Decision (Nathan, verbatim intent).** Graded — texture common, enterable
places rarer — with four riders:

1. **The aim is 1 per FACET, not 1 per square mile.** A facet is 1.126 km on a
   side and a square mile is 2.04 of them, so this doubles the target.
2. **Enterable places keep being added** "until I've run out of ideas". It is
   entirely possible most facets, or *all* facets, end up carrying an enterable
   feature.
3. **Do not rule that out constitutionally.** No mechanism may structurally cap
   enterable density.
4. **Per-kind frequency must be tunable, relatively and absolutely,**
   "individually and severally", as kinds keep being added.

**Consequence the campaign must carry.** The published gap figure moves:
84,200x, not the 41,200x now in `book/src/open-questions.md`, which was written
against the square-mile framing on 2026-09-03. **Re-scoring that Gradient entry
is part of this campaign's Definition of Done** (decision 0030).

*ideonomy passes / overturns:* 0 / 0 — a direct question to Nathan whose answer
is his intent, not a proposition to expand. Riders 3 and 4 became hard
constraints on entry #2, where a pass was run.

*Capture:* this entry; the DoD note above; constraint carried into #2.

### #2 [G1] — the mechanism is per-kind prevalence fields

**Question.** How does a facet acquire a derived feature? Three candidates:
(A) per-kind continuous prevalence fields sampled per facet; (B) one
significance scalar whose range is partitioned into kind bands; (C) multi-scale
— a mid-scale locality-character field conditioning per-facet detail.

**Decision. (A)**, with the modulating field as `Fbm` and discrete occurrence
drawn per facet from the prevalence.

**Why.** An ideonomy pass (cross-domain re-instantiation + combination, over a
lattice organon, dimension-prompts predictability/side-effect/autonomy)
collapsed the three candidates into one by ordering them:

- **(B) is (A) plus a constraint, and the constraint is the thing #1 forbade.**
  Partitioning one scalar's range into kind-bands forces the per-kind
  prevalences to sum to 1 — a simplex constraint — so raising enterable density
  necessarily lowers something else and "individually and severally" becomes
  unsatisfiable *by construction*. B is the obvious design and it is
  disqualified structurally, not by taste.
- **(C) is already inside (A) for free.** A mid-scale locality character is a
  multi-octave modulating field, and `Fbm::new(seed, octaves)`
  (`kernel/src/noise.rs`) ships today. There is no second mechanism to build.

*Precedent cited.* `cave_process` -> `presence_prob`
(`domains/terrain/src/features.rs`) is already exactly (A) at **vertex**
resolution: proneness from macro state, modulated, then a presence probability.
The campaign moves an established pattern down two orders of magnitude rather
than inventing one. `blend_at` (`windows/locale/src/lib.rs`) supplies macro
state continuously at facet resolution, and The Hachure (Stage 1) has just
shipped the same continuous-below-the-grid move for height.

*Alternatives discarded.* (B) — see above, structural. (C) — not discarded but
**absorbed**: it is (A) with octaves.

*ideonomy passes / overturns:* **1 / 0.** No overturn; substantial enrichment.
Three design elements appeared that none of the three candidates had:

1. **Correlation length is a first-class per-kind parameter, expressed in
   facets-of-travel** (from weaving: a motif repeating below the eye's span
   reads as texture, at it as rhythm, above it as pattern). It is separate from
   frequency and decides whether a kind reads as ground cover or as landmark.
2. **The legibility metric can be a standard spatial-autocorrelation statistic**
   (Moran's I / Ripley's K) rather than a bespoke one — from epidemiological
   prevalence mapping, which already separates a continuous prevalence surface
   from discrete incidence draws exactly as (A) does.
3. **"Feels found" has an exact, measurable meaning.** Registry row
   `KNOW-uncertainty-is-epistemic` already settles that in a deterministic world
   every doubt is incomplete observation, never a dice roll. So a place feels
   *found* iff **a knowledgeable observer could have predicted it from visible
   signs and a naive one could not** — predictability-from-antecedents, not
   variety.

*Capture:* this entry. Elements 1-3 go into the spec's design section.

### #3 [Q, Nathan] — density is the deliverable, legibility is the success test

**Question.** The same ideonomy pass's side-effect swap surfaced a reframe:
density is the intended effect, but the byproduct is that derived features
become **signs** of macro state, making the 120 km-resolution world inferable at
walking scale — which is the defect `SURF-biome-monotony-is-one-defect-at-three-sites`
seen from the other end. Byproduct, or the actual point? No precedent; the three
answers reorder the campaign completely.

**Decision (Nathan).** Option 1 — **build the density; preregister
sign-legibility as the test that distinguishes found from extruded.** Nathan:
"I agree both are very important, and I definitely want to pursue the legibility
aspect, but I agree that it can wait."

**Why.** It delivers the density that was asked for while making the bet
falsifiable in a shape the repo already owns — The Ford's
`channel-band-monotonicity` is literally "the position-continuous-noise guard
stated as a measurement", paired with the anti-vacuity companion
`channel-transect-dry-reach` so a degenerate world cannot score 1.0. Adopting
that pair costs little because the instrument exists.

*Alternatives discarded.* Option 2 (density only, no inference metric) — leaves
the campaign unable to tell wallpaper from signs, which is the one thing that
can make it fail. Option 3 (legibility as the point, density as the means) —
**explicitly not rejected**, deferred: Nathan calls it "a strongly desired
followup". It inverts the campaign into an epistemics project and would push
the density work out substantially; it is the natural successor once the
surface exists.

(1 pass); no separate pass was run for the disposition, since the fork was
Nathan's to settle and he settled it.

### #4 [G2] — a residency window over the function, and features by composition

**Question.** Where does the derived surface's cost actually go, and what holds
a materialized feature between derivation and use?

**Decision.** Three layers, and the middle one is new to the design:

| layer | what it is | lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the function | per-kind prevalence x occurrence, pure in `(seed, level, facet, kind)` | none — recomputed at will |
| the residency window | materialized feature COMPONENTS for facets near the observer | bounded; evicted when it stops earning its keep |
| the ledger | untouched | — |

A feature kind is a **bundle of components**, never a subclass. Discovery is
observation-scoped (proximity, visit, or rumor), so evaluation is bounded by
what is observable rather than by the world.

**Why (precedent cited).**

- **Composition is the constitution one level down.** CLAUDE.md: *"Adding a
  domain must never require editing an existing one."* A component bundle makes
  adding feature kind N+1 an append that touches nothing existing — which is
  exactly what #1's rider 4 demands (keep adding kinds indefinitely, tune each
  individually and severally). Inheritance would make every new kind a
  negotiation with the existing taxonomy.
- **Lazy, observation-scoped evaluation is not an optimisation.**
  `KNOW-uncertainty-is-epistemic` establishes that the world is a total function
  and an observer is a partial evaluation of it. Evaluating only what is
  observed *is* what observation means here; the performance win and the
  epistemics are the same fact.
- **The store already exists and eviction is already proven safe.**
  `kernel/src/derived.rs`'s `Derived<K, V>` with `Validity::Pure` — and the doc
  states our exact case: *"World-derived is not a third class — it is `Pure`
  with the world's identity … folded into the key."* It carries `evict(key)` and
  `evict_all()`, and a **chaos-eviction property battery** already evicts at
  every legal opportunity to prove correctness is unaffected. So a window under
  any eviction policy is safe by construction, with the battery pinning it.
- **The prohibition that still binds.** The Terrier: *"do NOT memoize inside
  this function, because a hidden cache in a derivation path is how derived
  state stops being derived."* Its prescription (hoist to the caller) is what
  shipped and the prohibition stands. Caller-owned, `&mut`-threaded, no
  `RefCell`/global/`OnceLock`, and a test pinning cache-present byte-identical
  to cache-absent — the contract `blend_at_cached` already states.

**The cost model this buys, to be preregistered as a budget.** Per-tick tax is
**zero** when the observer does not move (the window is unchanged, nothing
re-derives). Movement tax is **perimeter, not area**: stepping one facet evicts
a trailing edge and fills a leading one, ~84 facets per step at radius 10
against ~441 held. That ratio is what decides whether the radius is 10 or 40, so
it is a measurement, not a guess.

**Rumors are degraded observations, never fabrications (Nathan).** In-game
rumors must not become wild goose chases. The epistemic frame supplies the
generator: a rumor descends from an actual observation, worn by transmission, so
its error modes are **stale**, **vague** (precision decays per hop, yielding a
region rather than a point) and **misattributed** — never "rolled false". The
existing seam is already correct for this: `windows/vessel/src/knowledge.rs`
splits projection-derived entries (re-derivable, enforced by
`knowledge_is_subset`) from **heard** entries, which are *deliberately* outside
that contract because "heard != true is the epistemic point" (The Echo). A
rumor is a heard entry and is already permitted to be wrong. `Knowledge` is
per-agent and serialized into session snapshots, **not** the ledger, so
discovery state has a sanctioned non-ledger home today.

**Invariant to preregister:** a rumor names a REGION, and the region is never
empty. Following one always yields something; the failure mode is "not what you
were promised", never "nothing at all". That is the anti-frustration guarantee
stated as a test rather than left to tuning.

**Constraint banked for whenever a general component store is built.**
Component storage **must not be keyed on `TypeId`**. `derived.rs` gives the
reason it is one map per shape: *"`TypeId`'s ordering is not build-stable … a
`BTreeMap<TypeId, _>` would put an unstable iteration order under a
byte-identity guarantee."* Explicitly registered stable component ids instead.
Cheap now; discovered otherwise as a byte-identity failure reproducing on one
host and not another.

**Two errors of mine this entry corrects, recorded because a correction is
unaudited text.**

1. I claimed *"derived features cannot live in an ECS, because an ECS
   enumerates a materialized population and the point of derivation is that the
   population is never materialized."* Wrong: the point of derivation is to
   avoid paying **before the need exists**, not to forbid holding the result. A
   bounded window is consistent with derivation, and "cheaper to keep than to
   regenerate" is the right test. Nathan's correction.
2. I implied `Derived` was append-only and therefore unsuitable as a window. It
   has `evict`/`evict_all` and a battery proving eviction-safety. I read
   `RoomMeshMemo`'s append-only USAGE as a property of the type. Caught by
   grepping the type before asserting — which is the only reason it did not
   reach the spec.

*Alternatives discarded.* A hidden cache inside the derivation (forbidden by
The Terrier, and it is how derived state stops being derived). An unbounded
memo with no eviction (a walking session's key space is unbounded, so it leaks).

*ideonomy passes / overturns:* **0 / 0, stated explicitly rather than left
blank.** This is a G2 section, not a G1 or a nontrivial `Q`, so the
per-decision pass is not mandated. It also arrived through Nathan overturning my
framing directly, which did the adversarial work a pass would have done — and
the record shows it overturned two of my claims, not zero.
