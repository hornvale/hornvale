#3 [Q] — What is "the level" for a center of gravity when the level's own
passage graph is in pieces? · **The plan's metric: shortest paths through
any level, summed over this level's nodes** · Why: Alexander's center of
gravity is over "the spaces the group occupies", and a resident walking from
one island to another does walk up the stair and down again — the distance
is real, and the plan is connected by construction (0566) while a level is
not; a within-level sum crowns a small island by arithmetic, which the
drawing showed and the numbers confirmed (29% of hearts on the arrival node
under the wrong metric, 0% under the right one) · Discarded: per-component
hearts (several halls per level — a level with three islands is one house
in three wings, not three houses, and the tenancy is one people); the
largest component only (discards rooms the resident does use); a
whole-descent heart (one hall for five rungs is not what 129 describes, and
the Made rung is one level) · Ideonomy: 1 pass (organon-construction — the
companion screen IS the pass: drawing one real level forced the island into
view, which no histogram had; overturn of the draft metric, not of the
design) · Capture: the components-per-level histogram becomes a §4.4
report-only readout; the fact itself ("on one floor you often cannot reach
every room without going up one") goes to the Crosscut chronicle's
freshness sweep at close and to `MAP-underworld-viewport`'s neighbour row
as context.

  0%   a species NAME in prose ("something moves in the dark")

### Dimensions surfaced (and where each went)

- decomposability: three deliverables, one coupling — the reading is
  observable only through the writer, the hoarder is independent of both →
  task order: probe, writer, reading, hoarder, walks, book.
- predictability: everything here is derived; the one draw in play
  (`character_of`) is left where it is, so no plan byte moves for any input
  reachable today → the regression pin: all-Found origins produce today's
  plan byte for byte.
- materiality: the two scales above.
- intentionality: a people does not DESIGN the graph (the rock and the draws
  did); it READS the graph and assigns use — use follows form, which is the
  metaplan's keystone restated ("one skeleton, two readings") → the reading
  is a function of the plan, never a rewrite of it.
- modularity: the `Use` assignment is a worldgen module over `DescentPlan`
  with no vessel dependency, so The Precincts can read the same function
  over a surface district graph → it lives in `windows/worldgen`, not the
  vessel.
