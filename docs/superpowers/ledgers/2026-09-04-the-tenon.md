# The Tenon — decision ledger

Campaign C of the `MAP-one-kind-model` arc: **kind-to-kind edges**, with the
sleep-site preference as the named consumer.

Decision block reserved: **0726–0735** (`make decision-block NAME=the-tenon`,

#1 [Q] — **Campaign name: The Tenon.** A tenon is the projecting half of a
mortise-and-tenon joint: it holds because two pieces were each cut to suit the
other, and *neither piece names the other* — which is exactly the M+N property
the chosen edge shape has. `tenon` has zero occurrences anywhere in the tree
(`git grep -il tenon` → no matches), and no `the-tenon` entry exists in
`docs/retrospectives/` or `book/src/chronicle/`. `the-mortise` and `the-scarf`
were both checked and are taken (2 files each). No ideonomy pass — a name is
not a design decision.

#2 [G1] — **Scope: this campaign adds sleepable kinds AND the edge, and the
epoch is accepted.** Nathan's call, brought to him under the brief's explicit
instruction (a fidelity/scope decision) rather than resolved from precedent.

The evidence assembled for the call, all measured rather than reasoned:

- `the-fireside-bed` is the sole `ObjectProperty::SupportsRest` carrier
  (`windows/vessel/src/affordance.rs`, `object_registry`), and it requires
  `built && cold` at both bands.
- `cold-built-room-share`: median **0.186**, mean **0.262** over n=1000 worlds
  (`book/src/domesday/settlement.md`). So ~81% of built rooms, and **100% of
  wild rooms**, contain nothing to sleep on at all.
- The wild band has exactly **two** patterns, `the-clearing` and `the-pool`
  (`windows/vessel/src/interior/pattern.rs`, `INVENTORY`); neither is
  sleepable.
- Whether any body in any world has ever taken an `Afforded` bout is
  **unmeasured** — decision 0697 records that the affect-trace byte-golden is
  blind to the afforded path because seed 42's traced window is entirely open
  ground, and `PSY-rest-site-is-a-tuning-indicator` records the reachability
  test as unbuilt.

Discarded: *kinds first as a separate campaign* (front-loads the epoch, but
splits one fidelity story across two merges); *mechanism only* (ships an
unexercised layer on an unexercised layer — the exact compounding The Pallet
warned against).

Ideonomy: 1 pass, 1 overturn — see #3, which is the same pass.

#3 [G1] — **Edge shape: derived from traits both sides carry (form B), not an
authored `(species, thing)` matrix and not a general typed graph. OVERTURN.**
Nathan's call, presented with a recommendation; the recommendation itself was
produced by the pass and is not what I came in holding.

Tuple drawn: operators `abstraction-lift` + `substitution`, organon `notation`,
dimension-prompts `distribution`, `cardinality`, `symmetry`. **One pass, one
overturn.**

**What I was going to recommend:** the frontier essay's stated target — a
general typed graph over `KindId` (`EdgeKind = RestsOn | GrowsOn | SeedsBecome`),
with sleep as its first consumer.

**What the pass produced.**

The `distribution` substitution priced a new sleepable kind under each form:
**39 rows** under an authored matrix, **1 row** under a derived function. The
arc's own thesis is that adding a kind must be cheap — The Wicket's proof was a
brazier reaching a real world through five data rows and no dispatcher edit —
so an authored matrix *contradicts the campaign arc it belongs to*. Measured
against the tree to check the house idiom rather than assume it: **103**
`ComponentStore<KindId, _>` tables exist under `domains/` and `windows/`, and
**zero** pair-keyed tables anywhere outside test code
(`grep '(KindId, KindId)'` hits only `interior/pattern.rs`'s own census test).
`ObjectProperty::Lockable`'s doc states the rule outright: *"Still M+N: the
lock declares what it requires and the key declares what it carries, and
neither names the other."*

The `abstraction-lift` found the comparator that settles the false dichotomy.
Lifted, this is *a bipartite compatibility relation between two open,
string-keyed classes*. Blood-type compatibility is an 8×8 table everyone
memorizes as a matrix and which two three-bit antigen vectors generate
entirely; matrix factorization makes the general statement — **an authored M×N
matrix of rank r IS r latent traits per side.** So "table or function" is not
an architecture choice, it is a choice of *rank*, and the question becomes what
rank this world needs. The project has already answered a structurally
identical question once: Pianka niche overlap in the coexistence packer
computes a pairwise compatibility from two `ResourceVector`s rather than
authoring it.

The `cardinality` substitution killed my own recommendation. A general graph
with one live `EdgeKind` is speculative generality, and `ObjectProperty`'s own
doc refuses exactly that shape: *"each variant exists because a shipped or
imminent verb gates on it, and a property no verb would ever consult was cut
rather than kept."*

The `symmetry` prompt surfaced the slot nothing in the tree declares, and it is
the reason form C would have been wrong rather than merely early: the arc's own
exemplars disagree about direction. `grows-on` is anti-symmetric, `family_of`
(the frontier essay's "degenerate case") is symmetric-transitive, and
*rests-on* is neither — the **fit** is symmetric (two shapes suiting each
other) while the **payoff** is asymmetric (only the sleeper gains). One
undirected `edges` abstraction would silently merge all three.

**And form B dissolves the layering problem the brief called the campaign's
sharpest structural question.** Under B no crate ever names both rosters:
`domains/thing` carries surface traits, `domains/species` already carries the
sleeper's two axes (`sleep_grade_registry`, decision 0697), and the combining
function lives in the consumer naming neither roster's keys. There is no
sibling-domain dependency to justify and no composition-root exception to
claim.

**What form B costs, stated rather than glossed:** *a dwarf prefers stone* must
fall out of traits; it cannot be asserted. A pair the traits get wrong has no
override. Nathan was shown that cost and chose B over `B + sparse override`,
whose second source of truth for one question is the exact disagreement
`domains/thing`'s own module doc records deleting a field to prevent.

Alternatives discarded, with reasons: **A** (authored matrix) — 39 rows per new
kind, and the catalogue-of-solutions shape `interior/pattern.rs` explicitly
refuses; **B + override** — two tables answering one question; **C** (typed
graph) — one abstraction, one live consumer.

Capture: form C is not dead, it is deferred with its blocking condition now
named (a second `EdgeKind` with a live consumer). An idea-registry row records
that, and the `symmetry` finding, at G3.
